Ownership Statement

Effective: May 7, 2026  •  Qiyadon

1. Company Identity

Qiyadon is a B2B pipeline execution service provider. For the purposes of this Ownership Statement and all client engagements:

  • Legal Name: Qiyadon
  • Business Type: B2B Pipeline Execution Services
  • Jurisdiction: Services are provided internationally; the governing jurisdiction is defined in each signed Client Service Agreement
  • Registration: Qiyadon operates in compliance with applicable business registration and service delivery regulations in its areas of operation

Qiyadon is the counterparty to all Client Service Agreements and the provider of all pipeline execution services described herein.

2. Content Ownership

2.1 Client-Provided Content

All content, materials, data, leads, pipeline information, CRM access, messaging guidelines, and escalation rules provided by the client to Qiyadon for the purpose of delivering contracted services ("Client Materials") remain the exclusive property of the client. Qiyadon is granted a limited, revocable license to use Client Materials solely for the purpose of delivering the contracted pipeline execution services during the engagement period.

Client Materials are treated as confidential and are not shared with any other client or third party, except as necessary to deliver the contracted services (e.g., CRM platform access for outreach purposes).

2.2 Qiyadon-Generated Content

All follow-up message templates, sequences, scripts, outreach strategies, and process frameworks developed by Qiyadon in the course of delivering services ("Qiyadon Methods") remain the intellectual property of Qiyadon. Clients are granted a limited license to use Qiyadon Methods solely in connection with the contracted services during the engagement period. Clients may not reproduce, resell, or sublicense Qiyadon Methods.

2.3 Jointly Created Content

In cases where content is developed jointly by Qiyadon and the client — for example, customized message sequences tailored to the client's brand voice, or outreach strategies co-developed for a specific campaign — ownership of such jointly created content will be addressed in the relevant Client Service Agreement. In the absence of a specific provision, jointly created content will be owned by the client, and Qiyadon retains a perpetual, royalty-free license to use anonymized learnings from such collaboration for service improvement purposes.

3. Intellectual Property

3.1 What Is Transferred to the Client

Upon full payment of all fees due under the Client Service Agreement, Qiyadon transfers to the client all right, title, and interest in any custom deliverables specifically created for the client and explicitly identified as "work made for hire" or "deliverables" in the Client Service Agreement. This includes final reports, customized pipeline analyses, and bespoke strategy documents prepared exclusively for the client.

3.2 What Remains with Qiyadon

The following remain the exclusive property of Qiyadon:

  • General pipeline execution methodologies, frameworks, and process know-how
  • Standardized message templates and sequence structures (not customized for the client)
  • Internal tools, software, and automation systems used to deliver services
  • Anonymized aggregated learnings and data derived from delivering services across multiple clients
  • Any pre-existing intellectual property of Qiyadon or its personnel

3.3 No Implied Licenses

Use of Qiyadon services does not grant the client any ownership rights in Qiyadon's underlying intellectual property, methodologies, or proprietary systems beyond the limited licenses expressly described in this Statement or the Client Service Agreement.

4. Data Ownership

4.1 Client Pipeline Data

All client pipeline data — including but not limited to lead lists, contact information, pipeline stage data, CRM records, outreach activity logs, response rates, and conversion metrics — is and remains the exclusive property of the client. Qiyadon does not claim any ownership interest in client pipeline data.

4.2 Data Use Limitations

Qiyadon will only access, process, or use client pipeline data for the purpose of delivering the contracted pipeline execution services. Client pipeline data is never used for any other client, shared with third parties (beyond what is necessary for CRM platform operations), or leveraged for Qiyadon's own marketing, benchmarking, or product development purposes without explicit client consent.

4.3 Data Retention and Return Upon Termination

Upon termination of the engagement:

  • Client pipeline data will be returned to the client in a standard, machine-readable format (CSV or native CRM export) within 10 business days of the termination date, upon written request
  • Qiyadon will retain a copy of the client pipeline data for a period of 90 days following termination solely for legal record-keeping and regulatory compliance purposes ("Retention Period")
  • At the end of the Retention Period, all client pipeline data will be securely deleted from Qiyadon's systems, unless Qiyadon is legally required to retain it for a longer period
  • Weekly pipeline reports generated during the engagement remain the property of the client and will be delivered in full upon request at termination

5. Work Product

The following work product ownership rules apply to deliverables generated during the engagement:

  • Weekly Pipeline Reports: Delivered weekly every Friday. These reports are the client's property and may be used internally, shared with the client's stakeholders, and retained indefinitely
  • Custom Pipeline Analyses: Bespoke analyses and strategy documents created specifically for the client are the client's property upon full payment
  • CRM Hygiene Records: Records of CRM updates, hygiene actions, and contact status changes are the client's property
  • Escalation Logs: Records of escalation alerts triggered during the engagement are the client's property
  • General Methodologies: Qiyadon retains ownership of general execution methodologies used across multiple clients, even if refined during the engagement with the client's account

6. Confidential Information

6.1 Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party to the other in connection with the engagement, including but not limited to: client pipeline data, lead information, pricing, business strategies, CRM configurations, and the terms of the Client Service Agreement. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Statement; (b) was known to the receiving party before disclosure; (c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information; or (d) is rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction.

6.2 Mutual Confidentiality Obligations

Both Qiyadon and the client agree to:

  • Maintain the confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care used to protect their own confidential information, and no less than reasonable care
  • Not disclose the other party's Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent, except as necessary to deliver the contracted services (e.g., CRM platform access, communication tools)
  • Use the other party's Confidential Information solely for the purposes of the engagement
  • Promptly notify the other party upon discovery of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information

6.3 Confidentiality Duration

The confidentiality obligations in this Statement survive the termination of the engagement for a period of 3 years, unless a longer period is required by law or specified in the Client Service Agreement.

6.4 Required Disclosures

Notwithstanding the above, Qiyadon may disclose client Confidential Information if required to do so by law, regulation, or court order, provided that Qiyadon gives the client reasonable prior written notice (to the extent legally permitted) and cooperates with the client's efforts to seek protective treatment of such information.

7. Contact Information

For questions regarding this Ownership Statement, intellectual property concerns, data ownership requests, or confidentiality matters, contact Qiyadon at:

  • Email: contact@qiyadon.com
  • Response Time: We aim to respond within 2 business days

Please include your company name, account details, and the nature of your inquiry to ensure efficient routing. For data export requests upon termination, please include "Data Export Request" in the subject line.

8. Policy Updates

Qiyadon reserves the right to update this Ownership Statement with 14 days' written notice to existing clients. Material changes will be communicated via email to the authorized contact on record. The terms of any signed Client Service Agreement will govern in the event of a conflict with this Statement.
