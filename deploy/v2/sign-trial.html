Begin Your Operational Evaluation

Qiyadon is operational infrastructure — not a SaaS widget. Here's how the evaluation works before any commitment.

Phase 1 — Activation
Hours 0–72: System Setup
Duration: 72 hours

We connect your CRM, activate outbound cadence, initialize workflows, and validate integrations. This is onboarding — you are getting operational, not being evaluated.

Phase 2 — Operational Evaluation
Days 1–14: Live System Evaluation
Duration: 14 days from activation completion

Your system runs live. Campaigns execute across WhatsApp, Email, and LinkedIn. You receive weekly operational reports. This is the real evaluation period — it needs the full 14 days to demonstrate value.

Phase 3 — Explicit Decision
Day 14: Shared Assessment + Scale Option
No automatic commitment

We assess together. If Qiyadon is working for you (≥3 of 5 criteria met), you receive a Scale offer you can accept or decline. Billing begins only if you sign a new paid agreement.

Before You Begin — What You Should Know

✓ No credit card required. The 72-hour activation window is free.
✓ No payment method is stored during activation or evaluation.
✓ No auto-renewal. No automatic conversion. No billing without a new signed agreement.
✓ The evaluation period is 14 days — not 72 hours. 72 hours is setup.
✓ If you qualify at Day 14, the Scale offer is valid for 72 hours. You decide.
✓ If you do not qualify or choose not to continue — you owe nothing. No follow-up pressure.
✓ All information shared is treated as confidential under the Client Services Agreement.
✓ If you qualify and choose Scale: no minimum commitment after trial. Month-to-month billing. Cancel with 30 days written notice.
✓ Every message Qiyadon sends is governed by rules you approve before any sequence goes live — and reviewed by a human Board for accountability.
✓ Connects natively with HubSpot CRM — and all other major CRM platforms.
