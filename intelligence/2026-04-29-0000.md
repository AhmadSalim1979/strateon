## 2. What Is Changing in the World

### Market Shifts

**The AI infrastructure cloud war is fully open — and the dynamics just changed.**
OpenAI on AWS means enterprises committed to AWS can now access OpenAI models without the Azure-first constraint. This doesn't mean Azure is irrelevant — it remains primary through 2032 and OpenAI pays a capped revenue share to Microsoft. But the narrative of "OpenAI = Azure" is dead. Amazon, Microsoft, and Google now compete equally for OpenAI as a customer. For enterprise buyers, the multi-cloud flexibility question is no longer theoretical.

**Anthropic's $30B ARR trajectory is real but the quality tension is showing.**
$9B to $30B in four months is extraordinary growth, but the Claude Code quality postmortem is the cost of that speed. When your ARR is growing at 3x in four months, every product surface is under stress. The Microsoft $30B compute rental deal (from November 2025) suggests Anthropic made large compute commitments to fund that growth — which means the margin structure is under pressure even as revenue scales.

**The AI IPO market has a narrative problem.**
OpenAI is in a "sprint toward IPO" and missed its internal targets at the worst moment for investor optics. The sell-off in cloud and GPU stocks on the WSJ report shows that the market is nervous about AI revenue sustainability. SpaceX's $1.75T IPO target and xAI's integration of Cursor are happening against a backdrop of growing skepticism about whether AI revenue growth can sustain the valuations being built on top of it.

### Technology Shifts

**The Nvidia-free AI stack is real and operational — but the capability gap is real too.**
DeepSeek V4 on Huawei Ascend is production-ready. China's AI labs can now operate outside the Nvidia stack, which matters as U.S. export restrictions tighten. But DeepSeek's own technical paper conceding a 3-6 month gap on standard reasoning is an important calibration point. The Nvidia-free stack works — it's just not frontier-equivalent on all benchmarks.

**Claude Mythos is a zero-day hunter — and it leaked via Discord within days of restricted access.**
Mythos can autonomously discover and exploit zero-day vulnerabilities in major OSes and browsers. That's why it wasn't publicly released. Within days of giving restricted access to partners under Project Glasswing, a Discord group had guessed the deployment URL and used a borrowed contractor credential to access it. The authorization failure mode: agents with dangerous capabilities are being deployed with partner-access security models that weren't designed for the threat level.

**The developer tool market is bifurcating into commodity and premium tiers.**
84% daily usage but only 29% trust production output tells the story: AI coding tools are widely adopted as a drafting and exploration tool, but the last mile of production deployment still requires human verification. The trust gap creates a market for tools that offer stronger correctness guarantees, auditability, and rollback capabilities — not just autocomplete.

### Business Shifts

**AI M&A with Chinese-origin companies is now a regulatory minefield at any stage.**
Manus was already integrated with Meta's Singapore office before the NDRC blocked the deal. Two co-founders couldn't leave China during the probe. The unwind cost — after integration has begun — is a genuine operational and legal burden. This isn't just about Manus. Any AI company with Chinese origin or Chinese founders considering U.S. acquirers faces a new category of risk that wasn't present 18 months ago.

**The AI governance talent shortage is becoming a hard blocker for EU AI Act compliance.**
Conformity assessors booked 12-16 weeks out ahead of the August 2, 2026 deadline. Companies that haven't started compliance work are at genuine risk of missing the deadline. The technical standards defining "compliant" are still being finalized, creating planning paralysis. The governance talent gap is acute — the people who understand both AI systems and regulatory compliance can't be hired fast enough to fill every company that needs one.

**The AI distillation issue is becoming a bilateral negotiating topic, not just a trade dispute.**
The State Department's global diplomatic cable naming DeepSeek, Moonshot, and MiniMax, issued weeks before Trump's scheduled Beijing summit with Xi, makes AI distillation a bilateral negotiating topic. This isn't just about trade policy — it's about where AI sits in the broader U.S.-China relationship. The risk: distillation becomes a lever in broader trade talks, with policy responses that could restrict open model releases or create new compliance burdens.
