## Strategic Correction — 72-Hour vs 14-Day Framing

**Problem identified:** The 72-hour window was being framed as the evaluation/trial period, collapsing a 14-day operational evaluation into a rapid conversion window.

**Correction applied:**
- **72 hours** = Activation/onboarding phase (setup, CRM connect, cadence init)
- **14 days** = Real operational evaluation (live cadence, artifacts, trust establishment)
- **Day 14** = Explicit Scale opt-in (new agreement required, no auto-renewal)

Three-phase lifecycle now canonical.
