## SECTION 3: WHAT CUSTOMERS WANT THAT QIYADON ISN'T DELIVERING

This is the most important section. These are the unmet needs that Qiyadon hasn't even acknowledged — which means a competitor who addresses them owns the conversation.

### UNMET NEED 1: Proof before commitment

**What customers actually want:** They want to see what they're buying before they hand over leads. A real report. Real messages. Real outcomes from real leads.

**What Qiyadon offers:** A template of a weekly report. A website that describes what will happen. No real output.

**Gap:** Qiyadon requires trust before evidence. Every day without a real client output widens this gap.

**What we'd build:** A live demo environment — a sample pipeline with 30 anonymized leads, running a full 7-day cycle, producing a real weekly report. Sharable under NDA. The prospect sees exactly what they'll get before they commit a single lead. This is a product feature we can build in 3 days.

---

### UNMET NEED 2: A human who owns the outcome

**What customers actually want:** When their pipeline has a problem at 9pm, they want to message a human who knows their name and their leads. Not a support ticket. Not an AI that says "I've escalated." A person.

**What Qiyadon offers:** "AI-powered execution system, supervised by a human Board." The Board is AI. The CEO is AI. There's no named human on the execution side. The "human oversight" is a layer of AI agents.

**Gap:** Qiyadon calls itself a "managed execution service" — the anti-tool, the anti-agency. But the "managed" part is AI, and the "human governance" is an AI Board. Prospects are buying accountability from a system they can't call.

**What we'd build:** A named Account Lead — a real human who owns each client's pipeline. Available on WhatsApp. Knows the client's leads by name. Introduces themselves on day 1 with a video message. This is a differentiator that Qiyadon structurally cannot match — because their entire operating model is AI-first.

---

### UNMET NEED 3: Speed to first value

**What customers actually want:** They want to see something working within 48 hours of signing up. Not a 7-day onboarding. Not "we'll start in a week." They want to forward a lead today and see it actioned today.

**What Qiyadon offers:** 7-day onboarding process. Day 1 is pipeline intake. Day 2 is CRM setup. Day 3 is sequence approval. Days 4–6 is when follow-up actually begins.

**Gap:** The fastest path to trust is showing the customer their lead being actioned in real time. Qiyadon's 7-day ramp is designed for process safety, not customer impatience. A customer who forwards a lead on day 1 and doesn't hear anything until day 4 will panic.

**What we'd build:** Same-day onboarding SLA. "Send us your first lead today and we'll have it contacted within 4 hours." The onboarding process takes 2 hours, not 7 days. We configure while the customer watches. First lead contacted same day. This is a service level claim that Qiyadon can't make because their AI system requires setup before execution.

---

### UNMET NEED 4: Pipeline clarity between reports

**What customers actually want:** They want to know the current state of their pipeline right now — not just on Friday. If a lead went hot this morning, they want to know before end of day, not in next week's report.

**What Qiyadon offers:** Weekly pipeline report on Fridays (all tiers). Growth/Scale adds daily brief (Mon–Fri). Scale adds a read-only real-time dashboard.

**Gap:** The "between report" gap is real. A VP Sales who wants to check pipeline status mid-week has no tool to do it. They're dependent on Qiyadon's daily brief or weekly report. This is acceptable for some buyers but a deal-breaker for others — especially those with fast-moving pipelines.

**What we'd build:** A client-facing pipeline portal — real-time, read-only — available at Growth tier (not just Scale). Leads are color-coded: Hot (red), Warm (orange), Cold (blue), Dormant (gray). Activity log visible. Last 5 touchpoints per lead. The VP Sales can check whenever they want without asking Qiyadon. Qiyadon only offers this at $1,500+/month minimum — we're offering it at $750.

---

### UNMET NEED 5: Easy trial with no lead minimum

**What customers actually want:** They want to test the service with zero risk. Send 3–5 leads. See what happens. Decide. Not commit 20 leads upfront. Not sign a contract. Not attend a discovery call.

**What Qiyadon offers:** 14-day trial, no credit card, no discovery call — but requires 20 leads submitted within 3 business days. "No commitment" is false framing because 20 leads is a real commitment.

**Gap:** The trial friction filters out the curious and leaves only the desperate. The best prospects — those with real pipeline discipline who want to evaluate carefully — won't hand over 20 leads to an unproven service.

**What we'd build:** Frictionless trial — 5 leads, 7 days, no minimum qualification. A prospect can start today with 5 leads and see a real report in 7 days. If they want to add more leads during the trial, they can. But the entry barrier is zero. We capture the curious prospect who Qiyadon loses.
