## BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms an integral part of the Client Service Agreement ("CSA") executed between Strateon ("Processor") and the Client ("Controller" or "Client"), collectively the "Parties."

The purpose of this DPA is to set out the obligations of Strateon as a data processor in relation to the personal data of the Client's leads and contacts, processed in the course of delivering the AI Pipeline Execution Service.

This DPA ensures compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and, where applicable, the UK GDPR and national data protection laws of EU/EEA member states.

All processing of personal data under this DPA shall be governed by this document. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the CSA, this DPA shall prevail with respect to data protection matters.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Defined Terms

In this DPA, the following terms shall have the meanings set out below:

- **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person ("data subject") as defined in Article 4(1) GDPR. In the context of the Service, this includes the contact details and pipeline interaction data of the Client's leads.

- **"Processing"** means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure, dissemination, making available, alignment, restriction, erasure, and destruction, as defined in Article 4(2) GDPR.

- **"Controller"** means the entity (the Client) that determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data. The Client controls the purpose of processing — following up on leads to drive sales — and determines the means.

- **"Processor"** means the entity (Strateon) that processes Personal Data on behalf of and under the instructions of the Controller. Strateon processes lead contact data strictly to deliver the AI Pipeline Execution Service.

- **"Data Subject"** means the individual whose Personal Data is processed. In the context of the Service, these are the Client's leads and prospective customers.

- **"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by Strateon to process Personal Data on Strateon's behalf in connection with the Service.

- **"Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Personal Data transmitted, stored, or otherwise processed, as defined in Article 4(12) GDPR.

- **"Applicable Data Protection Law"** means, as applicable: the GDPR; the UK GDPR (retained EU law version of the GDPR as amended by the Data Protection, Privacy and Electronic Communications (Amendments etc.) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019); and any national data protection laws of EU/EEA member states that apply to the processing of Personal Data under this Agreement.

- **"Service"** means the AI Pipeline Execution Service as described in the CSA.

### 1.2 General Definitions

All other capitalised terms used in this DPA have the meanings given to them in the CSA or, failing that, in the Applicable Data Protection Law.

## 2. ROLES AND STATUS OF THE PARTIES

### 2.1 Controller

The **Client** is the Controller with respect to the Personal Data processed under this DPA. The Client determines:

(a) The **purposes** of processing — i.e., following up with leads for sales engagement and pipeline management;
(b) The **means** of processing — i.e., WhatsApp-based follow-up as specified in the CSA;
(c) Which data subjects' Personal Data is included in the Service.

### 2.2 Processor

**Strateon** is the Processor, processing Personal Data on behalf of the Controller. Strateon shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Controller, as set out in this DPA and the CSA.

### 2.3 Prohibition on Independent Processing

Strateon shall not process, retain, copy, disclose, or transfer the Personal Data for any purpose other than the performance of the Service as described in this DPA and the CSA. Strateon shall not sell, license, or otherwise commercialise the Personal Data.

### 2.4 Accountability of Moosa

**Moosa (AI CEO, Strateon)** bears accountability for all data processing decisions made under this DPA. Moosa has the authority to bind Strateon to compliance obligations and to receive and respond to data subject requests and regulatory enquiries on Strateon's behalf.

## 3. SCOPE AND PURPOSE OF PROCESSING

### 3.1 Purpose

The processing of Personal Data under this DPA is carried out for the purpose of delivering the **AI Pipeline Execution Service** — specifically, automated and semi-automated follow-up communication with leads via WhatsApp, pipeline logging, status management, and reporting, all as detailed in the CSA.

### 3.2 Legal Basis

The Client is responsible for identifying and documenting the applicable legal basis for processing under Article 6 GDPR. The Client warrants that its processing of lead Personal Data is conducted on a lawful basis — typically consent (Article 6(1)(a)) or legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) — and that the Client has obtained, or will obtain, all necessary consents or authorisations required to pass such data to Strateon for processing.

### 3.3 Nature of Processing

Processing activities performed by Strateon include:
(a) **Collection and ingestion** of lead contact data provided by the Client at onboarding;
(b) **Storage** of lead contact data in Strateon's operational systems for the duration of the Service;
(c) **Automated and semi-automated communication** with leads via WhatsApp on behalf of the Client;
(d) **Logging and recording** of all interactions, status changes, and pipeline events;
(e) **Classification and status management** of leads within the pipeline system;
(f) **Reporting** of pipeline activity to the Client (weekly and ad hoc);
(g) **Retrieval and access** in connection with data subject requests or regulatory enquiries;
(h) **Deletion or return** of Personal Data upon termination of the Service.

### 3.4 Categories of Data Subjects

The Personal Data processed relates to the following categories of data subjects:
(a) The Client's sales leads and prospective customers;
(b) Contacts of the Client's existing customers, where such contacts are included in the Client's lead pipeline;
(c) Any other individual whose data is provided by the Client to Strateon for processing in connection with the Service.

### 3.5 Categories of Personal Data

The categories of Personal Data processed by Strateon include, as applicable to the Client's onboarding data:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Identity Data** | Name, job title, company name |
| **Contact Data** | Phone number (WhatsApp), email address |
| **Pipeline Interaction Data** | Message content, timestamps, lead status, follow-up history, escalation records |
| **Technical Data** | WhatsApp message metadata, platform interaction logs |
| **Commercial Data** | Lead source, deal value, product interest |

**Special category data (Article 9 GDPR):** The Client shall NOT provide to Strateon any Personal Data that constitutes special category data under Article 9 GDPR (including but not limited to racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data, or sexual orientation) without explicit prior written agreement and appropriate safeguards. If such data is inadvertently provided, Strateon shall promptly delete it upon becoming aware.

### 3.6 Retention

Personal Data shall be retained by Strateon for the duration of the Service plus an additional **twelve (12) months** following termination of the CSA ("Retention Period"), after which it shall be deleted or returned to the Client in accordance with Section 9 of this DPA.

The Client may request a shorter retention period by written notice, in which case Strateon shall delete the data within thirty (30) days of the end of the billing period in which the request is confirmed, subject to Strateon's legal and regulatory retention obligations.

## 4. STRATEON'S OBLIGATIONS AS PROCESSOR

### 4.1 Processing Instructions

Strateon shall process Personal Data only on the documented instructions of the Client as set out in:
(a) This DPA;
(b) The CSA;
(c) Any written specific instructions agreed between the Parties in respect of a particular processing activity.

Strateon shall immediately inform the Client in writing if, in its reasonable opinion, any instruction from the Client violates Applicable Data Protection Law. Strateon shall not be required to comply with such instruction until the Client has confirmed or modified the instruction in writing.

### 4.2 Confidentiality and Personnel

(a) Strateon shall ensure that all personnel authorised to process Personal Data under this DPA are subject to binding confidentiality obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA.
(b) Strateon shall limit access to Personal Data to those personnel who need it for the performance of the Service ("need to know" basis).
(c) All Strateon personnel involved in the delivery of the Service shall receive training on their data protection obligations before commencing work.

### 4.3 Use of Sub-processors

(a) The Client grants Strateon a general written authorisation to engage Sub-processors.
(b) Strateon shall maintain a list of approved Sub-processors, which shall be made available to the Client upon reasonable written request.
(c) Strateon's current approved Sub-processors include:
   - **WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.)** — primary communication platform for lead outreach
   - **[Cloud storage provider — e.g., AWS/MongoDB Atlas]** — pipeline data storage
   - **[Analytics/AI platform — to be confirmed]** — lead interaction analysis
   - **[Communication/notification tools]** — escalation notifications to Client

(d) Where Strateon engages a Sub-processor to carry out specific processing activities on its behalf, the arrangement shall be governed by a written contract containing data protection obligations no less protective than those in this DPA (Article 28(4) GDPR).
(e) Strateon shall remain fully liable to the Client for the performance of the Sub-processor's obligations under such sub-processing agreement.

### 4.4 Data Subject Rights Assistance

Strateon shall, taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Client in fulfilling the data subject rights listed below. The Client is responsible for managing and responding to data subject requests. Strateon shall provide reasonable assistance within the following parameters:

| Data Subject Right | Strateon's Obligation |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| **Right of Access (Art. 15)** | Provide the Client with a data export within 10 business days of written request |
| **Right to Rectification (Art. 16)** | Update pipeline data upon Client instruction within 5 business days |
| **Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Art. 17)** | Delete lead data upon Client instruction within 10 business days |
| **Right to Restriction (Art. 18)** | Restrict processing upon Client instruction; mark lead as "do not contact" |
| **Right to Portability (Art. 20)** | Provide data in structured, commonly used, machine-readable format within 10 business days |
| **Right to Object (Art. 21)** | Cease outreach to the relevant lead upon Client instruction |
| **Rights related to automated decision-making (Art. 22)** | Not applicable to the Service as described; notify Client if this changes |

### 4.5 Records of Processing Activities

Strateon shall maintain written records of all processing activities carried out under this DPA, as required by Article 30 GDPR. These records shall be made available to the Client upon written request and to the relevant supervisory authority upon request.

## 5. SECURITY MEASURES

### 5.1 Technical and Organisational Security Measures

Strateon shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Personal Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure, or access, as required by Article 32 GDPR. Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, such measures shall include, at a minimum:

#### 5.1.1 Access Controls
(a) Role-based access controls restricting access to Personal Data to authorised personnel only;
(b) Multi-factor authentication for all systems accessing Personal Data;
(c) Strong password policies and regular credential rotation;
(d) Automated access logging and monitoring.

#### 5.1.2 Data Security
(a) Encryption of Personal Data at rest (AES-256 or equivalent);
(b) Encryption of Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher);
(c) Pseudonymisation of data where feasible and appropriate;
(d) Regular security patching and update management;
(e) Network segmentation and firewall controls.

#### 5.1.3 Operational Security
(a) Endpoint security on all devices processing Personal Data;
(b) Automated malware and threat detection;
(c) Secure backup procedures with encryption;
(d) Secure disposal of storage media and devices.

#### 5.1.4 Organisational Measures
(a) Designated data protection oversight function;
(b) Regular staff training on data protection and security;
(c) Data protection by design and by default in all systems processing Personal Data;
(d) Incident response procedures, including a tested backup and recovery plan.

### 5.2 Security Assessment

Upon the Client's written request (no more than once per calendar year, and subject to appropriate confidentiality arrangements), Strateon shall provide the Client with a summary of its security measures and, where mutually agreed, complete a security questionnaire or share a relevant audit report.

### 5.3 Ongoing Security

Strateon shall continuously review and improve its security measures as part of its operational practice, taking into account evolving threats, technological developments, and regulatory guidance.

## 6. PERSONAL DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

### 6.1 Detection and Notification

In the event of a Personal Data Breach, Strateon shall:

(a) Notify the Client **without undue delay** and, in any event, within **forty-eight (48) hours** of becoming aware of the breach;
(b) Provide the Client with: (i) a description of the nature of the breach; (ii) the categories and approximate number of data subjects affected; (iii) the categories and approximate volume of Personal Data records affected; (iv) the likely consequences of the breach; and (v) the measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects;
(c) Promptly investigate the cause of the breach and implement remedial measures.

### 6.2 Communication

Notifications under Section 6.1 shall be sent to the Client's designated Data Protection contact via email to the address last designated by the Client in writing, with confirmatory notice by telephone where the breach is assessed as high-risk.

### 6.3 Regulatory Notification

The Client is responsible for notifying the relevant supervisory authority (e.g., the lead supervisory authority under Article 56 GDPR) where required. Strateon shall provide the Client with all information necessary to fulfil the Client's own notification obligations under Article 33 GDPR.

### 6.4 Data Subject Notification

Where the Personal Data Breach is likely to result in a **high risk** to the rights and freedoms of data subjects (Article 34 GDPR), the Client is responsible for notifying the affected data subjects. Strateon shall assist the Client in preparing such notifications.

## 8. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS — ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS

### 8.1 Primary Responsibility

The Client, as Controller, bears primary responsibility for responding to data subject requests. Strateon acts as a processor and shall not respond directly to data subjects except where specifically agreed in writing with the Client.

### 8.2 Escalation from Data Subjects

If a data subject contacts Strateon directly with a request relating to their Personal Data, Strateon shall:

(a) Acknowledge receipt of the request promptly and in any event within five (5) business days;
(b) Inform the data subject that Strateon is acting as a processor on behalf of the Client and that the request has been escalated to the Client;
(c) Forward the request to the Client's designated contact within two (2) business days;
(d) Not respond substantively to the data subject request without the Client's prior written authorisation.

### 8.3 Prohibited Processing

Strateon shall not process Personal Data for the purposes of:
(a) Profiling or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects on data subjects, without the Client's prior written informed consent;
(b) Building or enhancing profiles of data subjects beyond what is necessary for pipeline follow-up;
(c) Any purpose other than the delivery of the Service.

### 8.4 Lead Consent Management

The Client is responsible for obtaining and maintaining appropriate consent from data subjects where required by Applicable Data Protection Law, including:

(a) **Consent to be contacted via WhatsApp** — the Client shall ensure each lead has consented to being contacted via WhatsApp before their phone number is added to the Service. The Client shall maintain records of such consent;
(b) **Consent for automated processing** — where any element of the Service constitutes automated decision-making or profiling, the Client shall obtain the necessary specific consent or establish a lawful basis as required by Article 22 GDPR;
(c) **Right to withdraw consent** — when a data subject exercises their right to withdraw consent, the Client shall instruct Strateon accordingly, and Strateon shall action the instruction within five (5) business days.

## 9. RETENTION AND DELETION

### 9.1 Retention Period

Personal Data processed under this DPA shall be retained for the duration of the Service plus twelve (12) months after termination of the CSA ("Retention Period"), unless:

(a) A shorter retention period is agreed in writing between the Parties;
(b) Applicable law or regulation requires a longer retention period (e.g., tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations);
(c) A pending dispute, claim, or regulatory enquiry requires preservation of evidence.

### 9.2 Deletion Upon Termination

Upon termination or expiry of the CSA, Strateon shall, at the Client's election:

**(Option A — Return of Data):** Return all Personal Data to the Client in a structured, machine-readable format (CSV or JSON) within ten (10) business days of the termination date, and then delete all copies of the Personal Data from Strateon's systems and those of any Sub-processors within thirty (30) days; or

**(Option B — Deletion):** Delete all Personal Data from Strateon's systems and those of any Sub-processors within thirty (30) days of the termination date, and provide the Client with a written deletion certificate within forty-five (45) days.

The Client shall designate its election in writing at or before termination. If no election is made, Option B (Deletion) shall apply by default.

### 9.3 Residual Copies

Following deletion, Strateon shall use reasonable efforts to ensure that residual copies of Personal Data are removed from back-up systems within the next scheduled backup rotation cycle. Deletion from back-up media shall be conducted in accordance with Strateon's secure disposal procedures (ISO 27001-aligned or equivalent).

### 9.4 Exceptions to Deletion

Strateon may retain Personal Data beyond the Retention Period where:
(a) It is required to do so by applicable law, regulation, or court order; or
(b) A legal hold or regulatory preservation obligation applies.

In such cases, Strateon shall inform the Client and shall limit access to the retained data to only those personnel who are legally required to access it.

## 10. AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE

### 10.1 Audit Right

The Client may, upon reasonable written request (not less than twenty-one (21) days' prior written notice), audit Strateon's data processing compliance under this DPA. Such audits shall:

(a) Be conducted during Strateon's business hours (09:00–18:00 CET, Monday to Friday);
(b) Not unreasonably disrupt Strateon's business operations;
(c) Be subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations;
(d) Be limited to one (1) audit per calendar year, unless a Personal Data Breach or regulatory investigation necessitates an exceptional audit.

### 10.2 Alternative Audit Mechanism

In lieu of a dedicated audit, Strateon shall, upon written request, provide the Client with:

(a) A completed data processing security questionnaire;
(b) Copies of relevant certifications (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II) where held;
(c) A summary of the most recent internal audit findings relevant to the Service.

### 10.3 Compliance with Regulations

Strateon shall comply with all Applicable Data Protection Law in the performance of its obligations under this DPA. Where Strateon is required to register with or notify a supervisory authority in connection with its processing activities, Strateon shall maintain such registrations and provide evidence of compliance upon Client request.

### 11.1 General Authorisation

The Client grants Strateon general written authorisation to engage Sub-processors for the processing of Personal Data under this DPA. The Sub-processors currently authorised are listed in Section 4.3(c) of this DPA. Strateon shall update this list upon engagement of any new Sub-processor.

### 11.2 Sub-processor Obligations

Where Strateon engages a Sub-processor:

(a) Strateon shall ensure that the Sub-processor is subject to data protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA;

(c) Strateon shall conduct due diligence on the Sub-processor's security measures and compliance posture prior to engagement;
(d) Strateon shall notify the Client of any material changes to the Sub-processor's processing arrangements, and the Client shall have the right to object to such changes within fifteen (15) business days.

## 12. INDEMNIFICATION

### 12.1 Processor Indemnification

Strateon shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client from and against any third-party claims, fines, penalties, or compensation orders arising out of or related to:

(a) A Personal Data Breach caused by Strateon's failure to implement and maintain security measures as required by Section 5 of this DPA;
(b) Strateon's processing of Personal Data outside the scope of the Client's documented instructions;
(c) Strateon's failure to comply with its Sub-processor obligations resulting in a breach by a Sub-processor;
(d) Any claim by a data subject or supervisory authority arising from Strateon's violation of Applicable Data Protection Law in the performance of the Service.

### 12.2 Controller Indemnification

The Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Strateon and Moosa from and against any third-party claims, fines, penalties, or compensation orders arising out of or related to:

(a) The Client's failure to establish a lawful legal basis for processing (including consent);
(b) The inaccuracy, incompleteness, or unlawful provision of Personal Data by the Client;
(c) The Client's failure to respond to data subject requests within the legally required timeframe;
(d) The Client's failure to comply with Applicable Data Protection Law in connection with its use of the Service.

## 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

### 13.1 Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the same law as governs the CSA, as agreed between the Parties in accordance with Section 10 of the CSA.

### 13.2 Precedence

In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the CSA, the provisions of this DPA shall prevail with respect to data protection matters.

### 13.3 Amendments

No amendment to this DPA shall be effective unless agreed in writing by authorised representatives of both Parties. Strateon may update this DPA to reflect changes in Applicable Data Protection Law, regulatory guidance, or its own processing practices by providing the Client with thirty (30) days' written notice. If the Client reasonably objects to any such update, the Client may terminate the CSA without penalty within fifteen (15) days of receiving the notice.

### 13.4 Survival

The obligations of this DPA shall survive the termination or expiry of the CSA for the period necessary to enable Strateon to fulfil its deletion, return, and audit obligations.

### 13.5 Notices

All notices under this DPA shall be delivered to the contacts designated in the CSA. Data protection-specific notices (including breach notifications) shall be clearly marked as such.

### 13.6 Entire Agreement

This DPA, together with the CSA, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to data processing. This DPA supersedes all prior discussions, representations, or agreements relating to data protection.
