## 1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION

### 1.1 Service: AI Pipeline Execution

Strateon shall perform **AI Pipeline Execution** — a managed, end-to-end pipeline follow-up and lead management service — as follows:

(a) **Relentless Follow-Up:** Strateon shall initiate and maintain consistent, persistent contact with the Client's leads via WhatsApp, at intervals Strateon deems appropriate based on lead status, pipeline stage, and urgency.

(b) **24/7 Operation:** The service operates continuously, around the clock, seven (7) days a week, including public holidays, subject to applicable law and platform policies.

(c) **Accountable Execution:** Strateon accepts responsibility for the operational status of the Client's pipeline. If a lead goes dark, goes cold, or is lost due to a failure in follow-up execution by Strateon, that failure is Strateon's to own.

(d) **Lead Resolution Management:** Strateon shall manage the follow-up lifecycle of each lead — from initial outreach through resolution (closed-won or closed-lost) — and shall escalate to the Client when a lead requires human decision-making that Strateon cannot make.

(e) **Weekly Reporting:** Strateon shall deliver a structured weekly pipeline report to the Client, detailing lead status, follow-up activity, escalation flags, and pipeline health indicators.

### 1.2 Service Modality

All service delivery is executed via **WhatsApp** as the primary communication channel. All interactions with leads are conducted in the name of and on behalf of the Client, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## 2. SCOPE OF WORK

### 2.1 What Strateon Does

Strateon shall:

(a) Import and maintain the Client's lead list as provided by the Client at onboarding;
(b) Initiate contact with new leads within twenty-four (24) hours of their addition to the pipeline;
(c) Execute follow-up sequences according to Strateon's operational methodology, adapted as reasonably required for the Client's industry and sales process;
(d) Log all lead interactions, status changes, and pipeline activity in Strateon's operational systems;
(e) Produce and deliver a weekly pipeline report;
(f) Escalate leads to the Client when: (i) the lead requests to speak directly with the Client; (ii) a commercial decision is required; (iii) a contract or proposal is awaiting signature; or (iv) any other event occurs that requires human intervention;
(g) Manage lead status transitions (e.g., moving a lead from "hot" to "closed-won") subject to Client confirmation;
(h) Maintain a record of all pipeline communications for a minimum of twelve (12) months.

### 2.2 What Strateon Does NOT Do

Strateon shall **not**:

(a) Create or design the Client's marketing campaigns, inbound funnels, or lead generation strategy;
(b) Make legally binding representations or commitments on behalf of the Client to third parties;
(c) Access or process client data beyond what is necessary to deliver the Service;
(d) Provide legal, financial, or tax advice;
(e) Guarantee a specific number of leads, closed deals, or revenue outcomes;
(f) Manage the Client's CRM infrastructure beyond Strateon's own operational logging system;
(g) Make commercial decisions on behalf of the Client beyond routine follow-up classifications.

> **Clarification:** The Service is execution of follow-up, not the generation of leads. Strateon is responsible for what happens *after* a lead enters the pipeline — not for bringing leads in.

## 5. DELIVERABLES

### 5.1 Weekly Pipeline Report

Strateon shall deliver a **Weekly Pipeline Report** to the Client's designated email address every seven (7) days. The report shall include:

(a) Total active leads in pipeline;
(b) Leads contacted in the preceding seven (7) days;
(c) Leads escalated to Client and reasons;
(d) Leads progressed to next stage (hot, qualified, proposal, negotiation, closed-won, closed-lost);
(e) Leads that have gone dark or require special attention;
(f) Summary of follow-up cadence compliance;
(g) Any pipeline risks or anomalies identified by Strateon.

### 5.2 Ad Hoc Reporting

Upon the Client's reasonable request (no more than twice per calendar month), Strateon shall provide an ad hoc pipeline snapshot within two (2) business days. Excessive ad hoc requests may be subject to a reasonable fee agreed in writing.

### 5.3 Escalation Notifications

Strateon shall notify the Client immediately (within four hours) of any event requiring urgent Client action, including but not limited to: a lead threatening legal action, a lead requesting data deletion, or a security-relevant event affecting the Client's account.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 7.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the **Service Commencement Date** (the date Strateon confirms onboarding is complete) and shall continue on a month-to-month basis thereafter, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section 7.

### 7.2 Termination by Client

The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing written notice. Upon such notice:

(a) The Service shall continue through the end of the current billing period;
(b) No refund of fees already paid shall be issued, except in the case of termination for cause pursuant to Section 7.4;
(c) The Client shall remain responsible for all fees due up to and including the final billing period.

### 7.3 Termination by Strateon

Strateon may terminate this Agreement by providing the Client with at least **thirty (30) days' written notice**. During such notice period, the Service shall continue on existing terms and all fees shall remain due.

### 7.4 Termination for Cause

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if:

(a) The other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving written notice specifying the breach; or
(b) The other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases to operate.

### 7.5 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:

(a) All outstanding fees shall become immediately due and payable;
(b) Strateon shall provide the Client with a final pipeline handover report within ten (10) business days of the termination date, unless the Client requests otherwise in writing;
(c) Strateon shall delete or return the Client's personal data (lead data) in accordance with the Data Processing Agreement executed between the Parties;
(d) The following sections shall survive termination: 6 (Payment Terms), 7.5 (Effect of Termination), 8 (Accountability), 9 (Limitation of Liability), 10 (Governing Law), and 11 (General Provisions);
(e) Neither Party shall have further obligations under this Agreement after the effective date of termination, except as expressly stated herein.

## 8. ACCOUNTABILITY

### 8.1 Ownership of Pipeline Outcomes

Strateon acknowledges and accepts **operational accountability** for the follow-up execution of the Client's pipeline. This means:

(a) If a lead goes dark — defined as no contact made or received within the agreed follow-up cadence window — due to a failure in Strateon's execution, Strateon shall investigate, remediate, and report the failure to the Client within twenty-four (24) hours of detection;

(b) If a lead is lost due to a missed follow-up that is directly attributable to Strateon's failure, Strateon shall document the failure, issue a credit to the Client's account equal to the monthly fee for the applicable billing period, and implement a corrective action plan;

(c) Strateon shall not be liable for the *outcome* of a lead (e.g., whether a lead converts to a customer) — only for the *execution of follow-up* on that lead.

### 8.2 Limitation of Accountability

Strateon's accountability under this Section 8 does not extend to:

(a) Lead quality, lead intent, or leads provided by the Client that are outside Strateon's control;
(b) Outcomes resulting from the Client's failure to respond to escalations within the agreed timeframe;
(c) Leads that are excluded from the Service by written agreement of both Parties;
(d) Force majeure events (see Section 11.5).

### 8.3 Moosa as Accountable Decision-Maker

For all matters of operational accountability, decision-making authority rests with **Moosa (AI CEO, Strateon)**. The Client may escalate pipeline disputes to Moosa directly via the channels communicated at onboarding. Moosa shall respond to escalation inquiries within two (2) business days.
