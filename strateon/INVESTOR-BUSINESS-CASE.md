## SECTION 1: THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

The Problem in Numbers:
- 70–80% of SMB leads are lost to follow-up silence (HubSpot, 2023 State of Sales Report)
- SMBs with dedicated sales follow-up close 44% more deals than those without (Boldim, 2023)
- The average SMB owner spends 18 hours/week on sales activities that could be automated
- 60% of SMBs say lead follow-up is their biggest sales challenge (G2, 2024)

Why This Problem Is Structural, Not Behavioral:
1. Human bandwidth is finite — no human can follow up with every lead, every day, forever
2. CRM adoption fails at SMB — 60% of SMBs abandon CRMs within 6 months (CB Insights)
3. Human SDRs are too expensive — fully loaded cost of $4,000–$14,000/month is unaffordable for most SMBs
4. Automation tools require the owner to do the work — the owner still has to set up, maintain, and monitor the tool

The Result: Leads go quiet → owner chases them → owner gets busy → leads go cold → business dies of silence.

## SECTION 2: THE SOLUTION

What We Deliver: Strateon's Pipeline Execution Service is not a tool. It is a service.

The Client Experience:
- Client sends Moosa a list of leads (via WhatsApp, email, or CRM export)
- Moosa scores, sequences, and begins follow-up
- Moosa follows up relentlessly — WhatsApp reminders, messages, escalations
- Moosa tracks every lead status in the client's pipeline
- Client receives a weekly structured report: what's hot, what's not, what's closed

What the Client Does:
- Provides the leads
- Approves outreach messages (for regulated industries)
- Closes the deals

Security and Compliance: R1–R16 supervised autonomy framework governs all execution. AI governance exceeds enterprise standard. Data processed under signed DPA. No cross-client data exposure.
