1. Services

Qiyadon agrees to provide the following services to the Client:

  1. Pipeline Execution Service — automated follow-up across email and WhatsApp for the Client's sales pipeline, applied to all active leads in the Client's CRM or lead tracking system.
  2. Cadence Management — systematic outreach following a defined follow-up cadence (Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30) until the lead responds or is escalated.
  3. Escalation Handling — stalled leads (no response after 14 days) are flagged for client review and decision within 48 hours.
  4. Friday Pipeline Report — a structured weekly report delivered every Friday, covering leads contacted, responses received, escalations pending, and pipeline health metrics.
  5. Live Pipeline Dashboard — real-time visibility into pipeline status, updated continuously during business hours.
