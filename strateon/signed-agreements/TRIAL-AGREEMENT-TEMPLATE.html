2. What's Free

The pilot is free for 14 days. No payment required. If after 14 days the Client does not want to continue, they simply say so — no fees, no obligations.

3. Decision at Day 14

At the end of the pilot, the Client chooses one of two paths:

  1. Go ahead: Convert to a paid Grow ($750/mo) or Scale ($1,500/mo) plan. Qiyadon sends an agreement and Stripe payment link.
  2. Walk away: Qiyadon provides a final pipeline summary. No fees, no follow-up required.

The Client's decision must be communicated by email or WhatsApp within 48 hours of receiving the Day-14 report.

4. Data

The Client provides read access to their CRM or lead list. All lead data remains the Client's property. Qiyadon deletes or returns all data within 7 days of pilot completion unless the Client converts to a paid plan.
