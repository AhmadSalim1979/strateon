2.4 Exclusions. The following are explicitly not included in this Agreement unless agreed in writing:

  • Cold outreach creation — Qiyadon follows up with leads provided by the Client; Qiyadon does not generate new outbound lead lists or write cold outreach from scratch.
  • Deal closing — Qiyadon manages follow-up; the Client closes the deal. Qiyadon will not negotiate, discount, or close deals on the Client's behalf.
  • CRM migration or data cleaning — Qiyadon operates inside the Client's existing CRM; it does not rebuild pipeline structure or migrate data.
  • Marketing or lead generation — Qiyadon executes pipeline; it does not generate leads.
  • Legal, tax, or compliance advice — Client is responsible for the legality of its outreach in its jurisdiction.
  • Custom software development or bespoke integrations beyond those listed in Schedule A.
  • Weekend or public holiday outreach — follow-ups run Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in the Client's stated timezone.

2.5 Client Responsibilities. The Client agrees to:

  • Provide Qiyadon with timely, accurate, and complete information necessary to perform the Services, including a current lead list within 3 business days of the Effective Date.
  • Confirm that it holds all necessary consents and legal bases to authorize Qiyadon to contact and follow up with the leads provided.
  • Designate a single point of contact for all Service-related communications.
  • Respond to escalation requests and decision items within 4 business hours during the Client's timezone during business hours.
  • Provide CRM access or lead forwarding within 3 business days of the Effective Date; if CRM access is not provided, the SLA clock does not start.
3. Deliverables and Reporting

3.1 Weekly Pipeline Report. Qiyadon shall deliver a weekly pipeline report to the Client every Friday via WhatsApp, covering: new leads ingested, leads contacted, replies received, leads reactivated from dormant status, escalations requiring Client decision, and overall pipeline health score.

3.2 Daily Brief (Growth and Scale tiers). Growth and Scale tier clients receive a brief daily status update via WhatsApp on business days (Monday–Friday), covering: new leads, leads contacted today, replies received, and any escalations.

3.3 Strategy Calls. Growth tier clients receive one 30-minute strategy call per month with Qiyadon's leadership. Scale tier clients receive one 60-minute strategy call per week. Strategy calls are scheduled by mutual agreement within 5 business days of request.

3.4 Onboarding SLA. Contracts signed and client intake form completed → onboarding begins within 1 business day. Leads ingested and first outreach sent within the SLA for the applicable tier:

  • Starter/Growth: within 5 business days of onboarding start
  • Scale: within 3 business days of onboarding start

4. Payment Terms

4.1 Fees. The Client shall pay Qiyadon the fees set out in Schedule A. Fees are quoted in United States Dollars (USD).

4.2 Recurring Billing. Monthly tier fees are billed monthly in advance on the 1st of each month. Annual fees are billed annually in advance. Overage charges (if applicable) are billed in arrears on the 15th of the following month.

4.3 Payment Method. Payments are made via Stripe direct debit or credit card. Client authorizes Qiyadon to charge the payment method on file for all recurring fees and overages. If payment fails, Qiyadon will notify the Client and attempt to recharge within 3 business days. Service may be suspended if payment is not received within 10 business days of the due date.

4.4 Late Payment. Payments not received within 15 days of the due date shall incur a late fee of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance. Qiyadon reserves the right to suspend Services upon 5 business days' written notice if payment is 30 days overdue.

4.5 No Refunds. No refunds are provided for mid-month cancellation or for unused months. Pro-rata credits are not issued for partial months. Clients receive service through the end of the paid period.

4.6 Annual Billing. Annual plans are charged upfront. No refund is available after 30 days from the start date. The Client may upgrade tier mid-cycle; a prorated credit applies toward the next annual term.

4.7 Expenses. Pre-approved, reasonable out-of-pocket expenses are billed at cost. Expenses exceeding USD 500 require prior written Client approval.

4.8 Taxes. Fees are exclusive of all taxes, levies, and duties. Client is responsible for all applicable taxes excluding Qiyadon's income taxes.
