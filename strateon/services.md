# Strateon — Service Offerings

## Service 1: Business Process Automation

**What it is:**
I identify the repetitive, manual workflows that are eating your team's time and turn them into reliable, automated processes — freeing your people to focus on work that actually moves the needle.

**Who it's for:**
SMBs and mid-market companies in IT services, SaaS, or operations-heavy businesses where your team is spending too much time on tasks that should run themselves.

**What's included:**
- Full workflow audit: I map your current processes end-to-end and identify automation opportunities
- Process design: Architecture for automated workflows with clear logic, triggers, and escalation paths
- Tool selection advice: Recommend the right tools for your stack (Zapier, Power Automate, n8n, custom scripts)
- Implementation oversight: I design and document the automation; your team implements, or I can guide you end-to-end
- Handoff package: Runbooks, decision trees, and monitoring dashboards so your team owns it after launch

**Price range:**
Hourly: $75–120/hr | Fixed engagement: $1,500–$4,500 depending on scope and complexity

## Service 2: Customer Success Setup

**What it is:**
I build your customer success function from the ground up — or fix the one you have. That means the right workflows, CS tools, health scoring, and playbooks so your team can retain customers, spot risk early, and grow accounts.

**Who it's for:**
B2B service companies (SaaS, IT services, agencies) that want to build a structured CS function without the overhead of hiring a full CS team yet — or who have CS but are seeing avoidable churn.

**What's included:**
- CS maturity assessment: Where you are, what the gaps are, what to fix first
- Lifecycle workflow design: Onboarding → adoption → value realization → renewal/handoff
- Health score framework: Metrics that actually predict churn — not vanity numbers
- Playbook development: Escalation playbooks, renewal plays, at-risk customer recovery
- CS tool configuration: Setup guidance for tools like ClientSuccess, Totango, Zendesk, or HubSpot
- Dashboard design: CS team scorecards and executive reporting views

**Price range:**
Hourly: $85–130/hr | Fixed engagement: $2,500–$7,000 depending on scope and whether tool config is included

## Service 3: Service Delivery Audit

**What it is:**
I review your current service delivery operation — SLA performance, team structures, processes, tools — and give you a clear, prioritized report on what's broken and how to fix it.

**Who it's for:**
IT services firms, managed service providers, or internal IT teams that are struggling to hit SLA targets, dealing with too many escalations, or unsure where the gaps are.

**What's included:**
- SLA performance review: Current targets vs. actuals, root-cause analysis of breaches
- Process audit: Incident, problem, change, and request fulfillment processes against ITIL best practice
- RACI assessment: Who owns what — and where accountability gaps are causing failures
- Team health check: Workload distribution, skill gaps, staffing efficiency
- Tool and ticket system review: What's being used, what's being wasted, what's missing
- Corrective recommendations report: Prioritized action plan with effort vs. impact matrix

**Price range:**
Hourly: $90–140/hr | Fixed audit engagement: $3,000–$8,000 depending on team size and scope

## Service 4: Executive Reporting & Dashboards

**What it is:**
I build the reports and dashboards that give you real-time clarity on what's happening in your operations and customer base — so you're not flying blind in leadership meetings.

**Who it's for:**
IT services leaders, operations managers, and business owners who have data but can't see the story in it — or who are spending days manually pulling reports that should take minutes.

**What's included:**
- Requirements discovery: I learn what decisions you need to make and what data drives them
- Dashboard design: Clean, executive-level visuals (Power BI, Looker, Google Data Studio, or Excel-based)
- Data pipeline setup: Connecting your sources (ticketing, CS tools, financials) to a coherent reporting layer
- Report automation: Scheduled reports that land in your inbox without you lifting a finger
- Interpretation guide: A short doc explaining what each metric means and what to do when it spikes

**Price range:**
Hourly: $75–120/hr | Fixed engagement: $1,500–$5,000 depending on number of dashboards and data source complexity
