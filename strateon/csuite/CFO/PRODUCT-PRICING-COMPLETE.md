## Part 2: Overage Fee Structure — CONFIRMED

**Recommendation:** Overage fees should be set at the following rates. Rationale follows.

| Tier | Included Leads | Overage Rate | Billing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 50 | **$6/lead** | Monthly in arrears |
| Growth | 200 | **$4/lead** | Monthly in arrears |
| Scale | Unlimited | N/A | Not applicable |

### Rationale for Overage Pricing

**Starter — $6/lead:**
- At $300/mo for 50 leads, the implied cost per lead is exactly $6
- This creates a natural price signal: hitting 50 leads means you should consider Growth
- The overage is not punitive — it's simply the marginal cost at the tier's implied rate
- Client who occasionally exceeds 50 by a few leads pays a small premium; it's not a trap

**Growth — $4/lead:**
- Lower overage than Starter because higher-volume clients get better unit economics
- At $750/mo for 200 leads, implied cost is $3.75/lead; the $4 overage is a modest premium
- $4 positions Growth as meaningfully better value than paying Starter overage rates
- Creates a clear upgrade incentive: at 50+ leads, Starter + overage is more expensive than Growth

**Design principle:** The overage structure guides clients toward the right tier rather than punishing them. A client at 55 Starter leads pays $330 that month — still cheaper than Growth, so it only makes sense if volume is genuinely occasional. Once overage costs approach the next tier's price, the upgrade pays for itself.

## Part 3: Annual Discount Pricing — RECOMMENDATION

### Recommended Annual Pricing

| Tier | Monthly (MoM) | Annual Rate | Monthly Equivalent | Savings vs MoM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $300 | **$2,700/yr** | $225/mo | $75/mo (25%) |
| **Growth** | $750 | **$6,750/yr** | $562.50/mo | $187.50/mo (25%) |
| **Scale** | $1,500+ | Custom quote | Custom | Custom (see below) |

**Discount rate applied: 25%** — slightly above industry standard (typically 10-20%) to create stronger incentive for forward commitment.

### Why 25% (Not 15-20%)?

- Qiyadon is a managed service business with real cost of delivery per client
- The primary value of annual prepay is **cash flow certainty** and **client retention**, not just "pay upfront for a small discount"
- 25% annual discount = roughly 3 months free per year — strong enough to be a real purchasing argument
- Industry benchmark: Many B2B SaaS businesses at this price point offer 20% for annual, 10% for semiannual
- Going to 25% creates enough of a savings to make "should I go annual?" an easy yes for a client on the fence

**Break-even on Scale:** Scale tier has no fixed pricing, so annual discount must be quoted custom. Recommendation: **Quote Scale annual at a 20% discount** off the agreed monthly rate (e.g., $1,500/mo → $12,000/yr instead of $18,000), with the standard 3-month minimum still applying. This gives large clients a meaningful savings while preserving the custom quoting structure.

### Annual Billing Terms

| Tier | Annual Price | Billing | Refund Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $2,700/yr | Charged annually upfront | No refund after 30 days |
| Growth | $6,750/yr | Charged annually upfront | No refund after 30 days |

**Cancellation terms for annual clients:**
- 30-day notice period regardless of tier
- Unused months are not refunded (standard for annual plans)
- Client may upgrade tier at any time; prorated credit applies toward next annual cycle
