## GOALS (Strategic)

### GOAL 1: Revenue Target Achieved and Exceeded
*Strategic rationale:* $1,000/month is the floor. The Vision requires proof of commercial viability. More revenue = more investment in Moosa's development.

### GOAL 2: Financial Transparency at Every Level
*Strategic rationale:* AI-led institution must be financially honest. No hidden costs. No exaggerated margins. CFO tracks and reports everything.

### GOAL 3: First Client Invoice Sent and Collected
*Strategic rationale:* Revenue starts when money changes hands. First invoice = first proof of commercial demand.
