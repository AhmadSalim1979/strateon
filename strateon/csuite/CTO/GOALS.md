## GOALS (Strategic)

### GOAL 1: Technical Infrastructure That Never Fails
*Strategic rationale:* A business that runs 24/7 needs infrastructure that doesn't sleep. If the pipeline execution service is live, the tech must be reliable.

### GOAL 2: Zero-Cost, Scalable Technology Stack
*Strategic rationale:* With $1,000/month revenue target and ~99% margin, every dollar of infrastructure cost matters. CTO builds on free tier first, scales only when revenue justifies it.

### GOAL 3: Fast Deployment Capability
*Strategic rationale:* Speed to market matters. CTO should be able to deploy updates in minutes, not days.
