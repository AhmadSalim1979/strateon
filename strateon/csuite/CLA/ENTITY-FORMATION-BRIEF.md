## 1. Entity Formation Options

### A. Delaware C-Corporation

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Registered Agent** | ~$50–100/yr (Incorp.com, Harvard Business Services) |
| **Formation Cost** | $89 state filing + $50 agent + ~$150–200 charter docs = **~$300–350** |
| **Annual Compliance** | $300+ franchise tax (Delaware) + registered agent + registered agent |
| **Timeline** | **5–10 business days** via mail; **1–3 days** expedited (+$50–100) |
| **DIY Possible?** | Yes, via Delaware SOS online portal. Simple for a single-member C-Corp |
| **Service Recommendation** | Use **Incorp.com** or **Harvard Business Services** — both sub-$200 all-in for formation |

**Key Takeaway:** Delaware is the gold standard for US tech startups, investors are familiar with it, and it offers strong liability protection and a familiar corp governance structure. Main downside: franchise tax can get expensive at high revenues.

### B. Wyoming LLC

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Registered Agent** | ~$25–50/yr (Wyoming Registered Agent LLC, IncNow) |
| **Formation Cost** | $100 state filing + ~$25–50 agent + ~$50 operating agreement = **~$175–200** |
| **Annual Compliance** | $60 LLC annual report + registered agent renewal |
| **Timeline** | **1–3 business days** online |
| **DIY Possible?** | Yes, fully DIY via Wyoming SOS (online) |
| **Service Recommendation** | **Wyoming Registered Agent LLC** (~$25/yr) or **IncNow** — both cheap and reliable |

**Key Takeaway:** Wyoming is the cheapest US option with strong anonymity (no public disclosure of members) and zero corporate income tax. Best for: holding companies, indie projects, early-stage bootstrapped ventures. Not ideal if you plan to raise VC — Delaware is preferred by US investors.

### C. Pakistan (Private) Limited

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Registered Agent** | ~PKR 15,000–30,000/yr (through a law firm or SECP-approved agent) |
| **Formation Cost** | ~PKR 50,000–80,000 (SECP filing + Certificate of Incorporation + stamp duty) |
| **Timeline** | **4–8 weeks** — SECP e-services portal, but plagued by manual follow-ups and NOC requirements |
| **DIY Possible?** | Not realistically. Requires a local SECP-registered lawyer or filing agent. Documents must be attested, memorandum filed, Form A submitted |
| **Service Recommendation** | Hire a **Karachi/Lahore-based corporate law firm** (e.g., Cornelius, Lancashire & Co.; or any SECP-registered agent) — budget PKR 100,000–150,000 all-in for first year |

**Key Takeaway:** Operates with a physical office. Can employ Pakistani staff. Useful if Qiyadon is building operations inside Pakistan. Main downside: bureaucratic, slow, and SECP compliance is demanding. Also, currency risk on repatriating profits.

### D. UAE Mainland Company

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Registered Agent** | UAE requires a **local sponsor (LSA)** or **service agent** — typically AED 10,000–20,000/yr for a service agent arrangement |
| **Formation Cost** | Trade license (DED): AED 2,000–5,000 + LSA fees: AED 10,000–15,000 + visa costs + office space (virtual OK) = **AED 15,000–25,000 (~USD 4,100–6,800)** |
| **Annual Compliance** | Trade license renewal AED 2,000–3,000 + LSA fee renewal + visa renewals |
| **Timeline** | **2–4 weeks** if documents are in order |
| **DIY Possible?** | Partially. DED e-services allows some filings. But LSA arrangement is complex and usually requires a local PRO/agent |
| **Service Recommendation** | **Dubai Economy & Tourism (DED) + a licensed local sponsor/pro** — or use **Shenzhen-Based formation agencies** (DBM, Commit) for turnkey mainland setup |

**Key Takeaway:** UAE mainland is accessible and growing. Zero income tax, strong business ecosystem, access to GCC markets. Virtual office now accepted in Dubai. Best for: Middle East market entry, freelancers, and if you want a Dubai presence without Free Zone constraints.

## 2. Recommendation

**For Qiyadon at this stage: Start with a Wyoming LLC, then plan a Delaware C-Corp.**

**Rationale:**
- You're pre-revenue, potentially bootstrapped, and need something fast, cheap, and legally sound
- Wyoming LLC gets you operational in days at ~$200 all-in
- You can open a US bank account with a Wyoming LLC (ask your bank — some require Delaware)
- If you raise external investment, flip to a Delaware C-Corp (you can typically convert the LLC to a C-Corp in-state, or restate — cost ~$500–1,000 with a lawyer)

**If Qiyadon's primary operations are in Pakistan** → Form a Pakistan Pvt. Ltd simultaneously. It gives you legal standing to hire locally, open a local bank account, and fulfill SECP obligations.

**If you're targeting ME/A轮 Gulf market** → UAE mainland is a strong add-on 3–6 months out.

**Bottom line:** Don't wait for perfect. Form the Wyoming LLC now. Add Pakistan Pvt. Ltd if operations are local. Move to Delaware when a lead investor signals interest.

## 3. What Can Proceed Immediately vs. What Needs Legal Review

### ✅ Can Proceed Immediately (No External Legal Review Needed)
- Registering a **Wyoming LLC online** (DIY via Wyoming SOS or through IncNow)
- Drafting a **simple operating agreement** (template-based, not bespoke)
- Opening a **US business bank account** (with Wyoming LLC documents)
- Using a **virtual office address** in Wyoming (e.g., Anytime Mailbox, Physical Address)

### ⚠️ Requires External Legal Review
- Drafting a **founders' agreement** (equity splits, vesting, IP assignment) — get a startup lawyer
- **Delaware C-Corp formation** if doing a priced round (requires a lawyer for Delaware DGCL §251 board/capitalization setup)
- **Convert LLC to C-Corp** — requires a conversion filing and legal opinion on tax implications
- Any **investor paperwork** (SAFEs, PPAs, convertible notes) — use a startup-friendly firm (Orrick, Cooley, or Y Combinator's SAFE docs if simple)
- **UAE local sponsor agreements** — LSA arrangements are complex and one-sided; must be reviewed by a UAE-qualified lawyer
- **Pakistan Pvt. Ltd incorporation** if done improperly can create shareholder disputes — needs a local corporate lawyer

## 4. Next Steps — To Form the Recommended Entity

### Wyoming LLC (This Week — Do Now)

1. Go to **IncNow.com** or **Wyoming Registered Agent LLC**
2. Select "Form a Wyoming LLC"
3. Name check: `STRATEON LLC` (or check availability)
4. File online — $100 state fee + ~$25 agent = **$125–175 total**
5. Receive Articles of Organization by email (1–3 days)
6. Download and sign a ** Wyoming LLC Operating Agreement** (template from Notion, Orrick, or Harvard Business Services)
7. Open a US business bank account — **Mercury, Relay, or Bluevine** (all accept Wyoming LLCs)
8. Get an **EIN** from the IRS (free, 15 min online via IRS.gov)

### Pakistan Pvt. Ltd (Within 30 Days)

1. Retain a Karachi/Lahore corporate law firm (budget PKR 100k–150k)
2. Name availability search via **SECP e-services**
3. Submit Form A + Memorandum & Articles of Association
4. Obtain Certificate of Incorporation
5. Register with **FBR** (NTN) for tax filings
6. Open a local corporate bank account

### Delaware C-Corp (When Investor Signals — Triggered)

1. Engage a startup lawyer (Orrick, Cooley, or NeoLegal)
2. Form via Delaware SOS, or through Harvard Business Services
3. Authorize capital stock (e.g., 10,000,000 shares at $0.0001 par)
4. Issue founding share certificates
5. Adopt bylaws, meeting minutes
6. Open a Delaware-aware bank (Mercury works for C-Corps too)
