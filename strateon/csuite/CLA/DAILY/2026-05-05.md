## Scale 3-Month Minimum Commitment — Compliance Gap Confirmed

**Issue identified:** 2026-05-08 session (SESSION-STATE/2026-05-08-001.md)
**Status this session:** CONFIRMED — compliance gap remains open

**Finding:** The Scale tier requires a minimum 3-month commitment. This is NOT disclosed in:
- The trial agreement (`sign-trial.html`)
- The trial page
- Any existing post-trial paid offer page

There is currently no post-trial paid offer page in the codebase. When a trial client qualifies for Scale, they are not informed of the 3-month minimum before providing payment details.

**CLA Position:** This is a legitimate compliance gap. A client converting to Scale must be informed of the minimum commitment BEFORE payment collection. Without this disclosure, Qiyadon cannot enforce the 3-month minimum and may face disputes if clients cancel within the first month claiming they were not informed.

**Required disclosure language (mandated by CLA):**
> "Scale plan requires a minimum 3-month commitment. Month-to-month cancellation is not available for Scale. By entering your payment details you confirm acceptance of the 3-month minimum."

**Owner:** CTO/CMO — must build post-trial paid offer page with this disclosure before Scale clients can convert

**CLA sign-off required:** Before this page goes live, CLA must review and approve the disclosure language.
