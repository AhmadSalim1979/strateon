**Action:** Created formal consent chain documentation framework governing all client engagements.

**Deliverable:** `strateon/csuite/CLA/CONSENT-CHAIN-FRAMEWORK.md` (v1.0, 2026-05-05)

**Content:**
- **Minimum acceptable consent evidence by channel:** email, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, cold calling
- **Jurisdiction-specific standards:**
  - Pakistan: PDP Act 2018
  - UAE: PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021)
  - US: CAN-SPAM / TCPA
  - UK: UK GDPR + PECR
  - EU: GDPR + ePrivacy Directive
  - Germany: GDPR + TTDSG
- **Traffic light system:** GREEN / AMBER / RED consent chain assessment
- **Pre-Outreach CLA Sign-Off Checklist:** 10 items — no outreach begins until all checked by CLA
- **Opt-out processing SLA:** 4 business hours from receipt

**Governing principle:** Qiyadon does not send outreach on behalf of a client unless CLA has reviewed and approved the consent evidence. We do not take the client's word for it. We document our review.
