### Work Item 2: CONSENT-CHAIN-FRAMEWORK.md — Created ✅

**File created:** `strateon/csuite/CLA/CONSENT-CHAIN-FRAMEWORK.md` (13,482 bytes, v1.0)

**Purpose:** Formalizes Objective 2.2. Defines the minimum acceptable evidence of consent Qiyadon requires before executing any outreach on behalf of a client. Operationalizes GOAL 2 and GOAL 3.

**Document structure:**
- Definitions (consent, consent chain, data subject, client, consent evidence, lawful basis)
- Minimum consent evidence standards by channel (email, WhatsApp/messaging, LinkedIn, cold calling)
- Jurisdiction-specific consent standards (Pakistan, UAE, US, UK, EU, Germany)
- Consent chain documentation requirements (required from client)
- CLA review process (GREEN/AMBER/RED system)
- Consent chain RED LINES (absolute prohibitions)
- Opt-out mechanism requirements per channel
- Client responsibility declaration (per CSA Section 1.3)
- Audit trail requirements
- Pre-outreach CLA sign-off checklist (10 items)

**Key positions established:**
- Email: Written opt-in record required; double opt-in preferred; purchased lists = ❌ NOT ACCEPTABLE
- WhatsApp: Written client confirmation that leads consented; no purchased WhatsApp contacts
- LinkedIn: B2B context acceptable; connection alone ≠ consent; opt-out required in every message
- Cold calling: TCPA (US) requires express written consent for auto-dialed calls; UK/EU: prior opt-in required for marketing to individuals
- German engagements: no German client without external counsel review (Tier 3, Jurisdiction Comfort List)

**CLA review process:**
- Client submits Intake Form Section 5 (Consent Chain)
- CLA reviews → GREEN (proceed), AMBER (proceed with caveats), RED (do not proceed until remediated)
- CLA sign-off recorded in Intake Form Section 8

**Pre-outreach checklist (10 items):**
1. CSA signed by both parties
2. DPA signed by both parties (EU/UK — mandatory; all others — standard)
3. Client Intake Form completed, Section 5 reviewed
4. Consent mechanism assessed GREEN or AMBER (not RED)
5. Consent evidence reviewed and documented
6. Outreach script reviewed and approved by CLA
7. Opt-out mechanism confirmed in outreach script
8. Client approved outreach script before use
9. Opt-out log established and accessible
10. CLA sign-off recorded in Intake Form Section 8
