**CFO-2026-05-08-001 flagged:** Scale 3-month minimum not disclosed in trial agreement.

**Finding:**
- The trial agreement (`sign-trial.html`) is for the free 14-day trial. No payment is collected.
- The Scale 3-month minimum commitment would apply only when a client converts to a paid Scale plan.
- The trial agreement does NOT mention the Scale tier or its minimum commitment.
- There is no post-trial paid offer page in the codebase yet.

**Assessment:** This is a LEGITIMATE COMPLIANCE GAP. When a trial client qualifies and receives a paid offer for Scale, they need to be told about the 3-month minimum BEFORE they sign up for Scale. Currently there is no mechanism for this disclosure because no post-trial paid offer page exists.
