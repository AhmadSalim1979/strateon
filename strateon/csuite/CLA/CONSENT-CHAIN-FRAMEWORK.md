## Purpose

This document defines the minimum acceptable evidence of consent that Qiyadon requires before executing any outreach on behalf of a client. It operationalizes GOAL 2 (Jurisdiction-Right Service Delivery) and GOAL 3 (Accountability Claim Is Legally Sound) by ensuring every outreach is backed by a documented, lawful consent chain.

**Core principle:** Qiyadon does not send outreach on behalf of a client unless we have reviewed and approved the consent evidence. We do not take client's word for it. We document our review.

---

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Consent** | A freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of a data subject's agreement to their personal data being processed and contacted |
| **Consent Chain** | The documented sequence showing how, when, where, and under what circumstances a lead/prospect consented to being contacted |
| **Data Subject** | The individual whose personal data is held and who may be contacted (i.e., the client's lead/prospect) |
| **Client** | The B2B entity that has engaged Qiyadon to execute outreach |
| **Consent Evidence** | Any document, record, or log that demonstrates the consent chain for a given lead or cohort of leads |
| **Lawful Basis** | The legal ground for processing personal data, per GDPR/UK GDPR (consent, contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation, vital interests, public task) |

## Minimum Consent Evidence Standards by Channel

### Email Outreach

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Evidence required** | Written record of consent: opt-in checkbox, double opt-in confirmation, or documented legitimate interest assessment |
| **Opt-in checkbox** | Must be an unchecked by default, separate from T&Cs, with clear language explaining what the sign-up is for |
| **Double opt-in** | Preferred. Confirmation email sent to the address; recipient must click to confirm — creates a timestamped record |
| **Legitimate interest** | Only acceptable if client has conducted a documented LIA (Legitimate Interest Assessment) and the communications are B2B and not unwanted |
| **Consent record** | Must show: email address, date/time of consent, consent text shown, method (checkbox/other), source page/URL |
| **Bulk import of purchased lists** | ❌ NOT ACCEPTABLE — purchased or rented email lists do not constitute valid consent for CAN-SPAM or GDPR purposes |

**Minimum proof for email:** At minimum, a screenshot or export of the opt-in mechanism with date range and volume is acceptable for initial review. CLA reserves the right to require a full consent export.

### WhatsApp / Messaging Outreach

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Evidence required** | Client must confirm each lead has opted in to receive WhatsApp/business messages via that brand |
| **Single opt-in** | Acceptable if consent language was clear and specific to the brand and message type |
| **Consent record** | Client's CRM/system must log: phone number, date of opt-in, consent text, channel, source |
| **Third-party consent brokers** | Acceptable only if the broker can provide a compliant consent record per the client's jurisdiction |
| **No purchased WhatsApp contacts** | ❌ NOT ACCEPTABLE — WhatsApp Business Policy prohibits contacting users who have not consented |

**Minimum proof for WhatsApp:** Written confirmation from client (email or signed form) that leads have consented, with a description of the consent mechanism, satisfies our minimum. Full consent log is preferred.

### LinkedIn Outreach

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Evidence required** | LinkedIn's own connection + message functionality — connecting with a prospect on LinkedIn creates a business relationship record |
| **InMail/sales navigator** | Acceptable under B2B context if the outreach is relevant to the recipient's professional role and not unwanted |
| **Connection without consent** | LinkedIn connection alone is NOT consent to marketing messages — subsequent outreach must be relevant to the professional connection |
| **Opt-out mechanism** | Every LinkedIn outreach message must include an opt-out (e.g., "Reply STOP to opt out") |

**Minimum proof for LinkedIn:** Client's target list was sourced through LinkedIn's own tools or other B2B platforms with appropriate terms of service; outreach is relevant B2B communication. CLA review of outreach scripts is mandatory before first LinkedIn message is sent.

### Cold Calling / Telephone

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Evidence required** | For US: TCPA express written consent for SMS/autodial. For UK/EU: prior opt-in consent required before any sales call |
| **TCPA (US)** | If using auto-dialer or pre-recorded messages: express written consent (not just verbal) required — must be documented per FCC rules |
| **UK/EU** | Prior opt-in consent required for marketing calls to individuals (B2C); legitimate interest may apply for B2B in some EU states |
| **Consent record** | Must show: phone number, date of consent, consent text, method of consent, caller identity |

**Qiyadon position:** Qiyadon's current service does not include cold calling as a primary channel. If cold calling is added in future, this framework will be updated accordingly.

## Consent Chain Documentation Requirements

For each client, the Client Intake Form (Section 5) captures the consent chain. The following must be provided before any outreach begins:

### Required from Client

| # | Document/Record | Purpose | When Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Description of how consent was obtained | To assess validity of consent mechanism | Always — before outreach |
| 2 | Copy of opt-in text/checkbox shown to leads | To verify consent language was clear and specific | Always — before outreach |
| 3 | Consent record sample (export, screenshot, or log summary) | To verify consent was actually recorded | Always — before outreach |
| 4 | Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) if relying on LI | Required if not using explicit consent | If LI is the legal basis |
| 5 | Source URL/landing page for opt-in | To verify where consent was collected | Preferred — before outreach |
| 6 | Privacy Policy URL provided to leads | Required under GDPR/UK GDPR | EU/UK clients — always |

### CLA Review Process

```
Client submits Intake Form (Section 5 — Consent Chain)
        ↓
CLA reviews consent mechanism described
        ↓
CLA flags as GREEN / AMBER / RED
        ↓
GREEN: Proceed with outreach. Log CLA approval in Intake Form.
AMBER: Proceed with outreach — with written caveats from CLA. Client must acknowledge caveats.
RED: DO NOT PROCEED. Outreach cannot begin until consent chain is remediated and re-reviewed.
        ↓
CLA sign-off recorded in Intake Form Section 8
```

## Jurisdiction-Specific Consent Standards

### Pakistan (PDP Act 2018)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Lawful basis** | Consent required as primary basis for processing |
| **Consent standard** | Must be clear, specific, for a defined purpose; can be withdrawn |
| **Special category data** | Explicit consent required; separate from general consent |
| **Evidence** | Opt-in record or documented LI assessment |

### UAE (PDPL)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Lawful basis** | Consent-based framework |
| **Consent standard** | Freely given, specific, informed |
| **Evidence** | Opt-in record or documented LI assessment |

### United States (CAN-SPAM/TCPA)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **CAN-SPAM** | Opt-out mechanism in every email; accurate sender identity; no deceptive headers |
| **TCPA** | Express written consent for auto-dialed SMS and telephone calls; documented per-call consent record |
| **Evidence** | Opt-out mechanism in every message; TCPA consent records for SMS campaigns |

### UK (UK GDPR + PECR)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Marketing to individuals** | Prior opt-in consent required (PECR) — no legitimate interest carve-out for marketing |
| **B2B marketing** | Legitimate interest may apply for cold B2B email — but best practice is opt-in or clear consent |
| **Evidence** | Logged opt-in consent per PECR; LIA if relying on legitimate interest |

### EU (GDPR + ePrivacy Directive)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Marketing to individuals** | Prior opt-in consent required under ePrivacy Directive |
| **B2B marketing** | Legitimate interest may apply — but must have documented LIA and respect for recipient's expectations |
| **Special category data** | Strict restrictions; explicit consent required per GDPR Article 9 |
| **Evidence** | Documented opt-in or LIA; privacy notice provided to data subjects |

### Germany (GDPR + TTDSG)

| Requirement | Standard |
|---|---|
| **Cookie/data processing** | TTDSG requires consent for certain data processing activities beyond GDPR baseline |
| **Marketing** | Strict enforcement by DSK (German data protection conference) — prior opt-in required |
| **Evidence** | Explicit documented consent; TTDSG-compliant cookie mechanisms if website-driven |
| **Note** | No German engagements accepted without external legal counsel review — see Jurisdiction Comfort List |

## Consent Chain — RED LINES

The following are absolute prohibitions. No outreach may proceed under any circumstance without resolution:

1. **No outreach without signed CSA and DPA** — contracts must be in place before any work begins.
2. **No outreach where consent was obtained via purchased or rented list** — this is not valid consent under any jurisdiction's framework.
3. **No outreach to leads who have explicitly opted out** — opt-out requests must be honored immediately.
4. **No outreach without a working opt-out mechanism in every message** — this applies to every channel, every message, every time.
5. **No special category data** processed without explicit additional approval and separate addendum.
6. **No EU/UK/DE client** served without a fully executed DPA — see Jurisdiction Comfort List.
7. **No German client** served without external legal counsel review — see Jurisdiction Comfort List.

## Opt-Out Mechanism Requirements

Every outreach message sent by Qiyadon on behalf of a client must include:

| Channel | Required Opt-Out Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Email** | Functional unsubscribe link (one click) — e.g., "Unsubscribe" or "Click here to opt out" |
| **WhatsApp** | Reply STOP keyword OR WhatsApp's native block/reporting mechanism — client notified of all block events |
| **LinkedIn** | "Reply STOP to opt out" text included in every message |
| **SMS (future)** | STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE keyword — standard industry practice |

**Opt-out processing SLA:**
- All opt-out requests honored within **4 business hours** of receipt
- Opt-out requests logged with: date, time, channel, contact identifier, request type
- Client notified of opt-out requests within 24 hours

## Client Responsibility Declaration (Per CSA)

The CSA (Section 1.3) requires the Client to:
- Secure all required consents, licenses, and permissions needed for Qiyadon to contact or process data on the Client's behalf
- Confirm that all lead data provided to Qiyadon was collected in compliance with applicable laws

This framework operationalizes that CSA clause. Qiyadon does not take client's word alone — we review the evidence.

## CLA Sign-Off Checklist (Pre-Outreach)

Before any outreach message is sent for any client:

- [ ] CSA signed by both parties
- [ ] DPA signed by both parties (EU/UK clients — mandatory; all other clients — standard)
- [ ] Client Intake Form completed, Section 5 (Consent Chain) reviewed
- [ ] Consent mechanism assessed as GREEN or AMBER (not RED)
- [ ] Consent evidence reviewed and documented in Intake Form Section 8
- [ ] Outreach script reviewed and approved by CLA
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism confirmed in outreach script
- [ ] Client approved the outreach script before use
- [ ] Opt-out log established and accessible
- [ ] CLA sign-off recorded in Intake Form Section 8

**No outreach begins until all items are checked.**
