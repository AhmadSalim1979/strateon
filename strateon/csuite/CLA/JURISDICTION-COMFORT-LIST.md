## Purpose

This document defines which jurisdictions Qiyadon can serve immediately, which require additional safeguards, and which require external legal review before any engagement. It operationalizes GOAL 2: "Jurisdiction-Right Service Delivery."

This is a living document. CLA updates this list as laws change or as new jurisdictions are assessed.

---

## TIER 1 — Serve Immediately

Clients in these jurisdictions can be onboarded with standard CSA + DPA, subject to completion of the Client Intake Form.

### 🇵🇰 Pakistan
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | PDP Act 2018 (Pakistan Data Protection Act), e-Commerce Ordinance 2002 |
| **Status** | ✅ Serve immediately |
| **Requirements** | Standard DPA; client confirms consent basis for outreach |
| **Consent standard** | Consent-based; client must document how/when consent was obtained |
| **Risk level** | Medium |
| **Notes** | PDP Act 2018 parallels GDPR but with fewer enforcement precedents. Data minimization and purpose limitation apply. No special category data without explicit additional approval. |

### 🇦🇪 United Arab Emirates (UAE)
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | UAE PDPL (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021), Cybercrime Law |
| **Status** | ✅ Serve immediately |
| **Requirements** | Standard DPA; local sponsor arrangement may be required for certain license types |
| **Consent standard** | Consent-based; PDPL requires clear consent for data processing |
| **Risk level** | Medium |
| **Notes** | Dubai mainland requires local sponsor (LSA) if physical presence is needed. Remote service delivery is generally permissible. DPA must reference PDPL. |

### 🇺🇸 United States (Federal)
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | CAN-SPAM Act, TCPA (for SMS/auto-dialing), FTC Act, emerging AI regulations |
| **Status** | ✅ Serve immediately |
| **Requirements** | Standard DPA; opt-out mechanism mandatory in all outreach messages; no auto-dialed SMS without TCPA consent |
| **Consent standard** | CAN-SPAM: opt-out mechanism required in all commercial emails. TCPA: express written consent for SMS/autodial. |
| **Risk level** | Medium-High |
| **Notes** | CAN-SPAM applies to commercial email. TCPA is specific to SMS and auto-dialed calls. State laws (e.g., CCPA in California) may add requirements for residents of those states. Standard DPA covers data protection; outreach channels must comply separately. |

## TIER 2 — Conditional Service (Additional Safeguards Required)

Clients in these jurisdictions may be served, but only with a fully executed DPA and additional jurisdiction-specific review.

### 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | UK GDPR, PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations), Data Protection Act 2018 |
| **Status** | ⚠️ Conditional — DPA mandatory, external review recommended |
| **Requirements** | Full DPA (UK GDPR-compliant); client confirms ICO registration; consent chain documented per PECR |
| **Consent standard** | PECR requires explicit consent for marketing emails/texts to individuals; consent must be logged |
| **Risk level** | High |
| **Notes** | PECR marketing rules are strict. Marketing to individuals requires prior opt-in consent — not just legitimate interests. ICO as supervisory authority. UK GDPR post-Brexit is substantively similar to EU GDPR. External legal review strongly recommended before first UK client. |
| **External review required?** | Yes — before first UK engagement |

### 🇪🇺 European Union (General)
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | GDPR, EU AI Act (phased), ePrivacy Directive |
| **Status** | ⚠️ Conditional — DPA mandatory, external review recommended |
| **Requirements** | Full GDPR DPA; client confirms lawful basis for processing; no special category data; AI Act disclosure if applicable |
| **Consent standard** | GDPR: lawful basis required (consent, legitimate interest, or contract). Marketing to individuals in EU requires consent per ePrivacy Directive. Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous. |
| **Risk level** | High |
| **Notes** | DPA with GDPR-compliant clauses is non-negotiable before any EU work begins. Each EU member state may have additional national law overlay. External legal review is strongly recommended before first EU engagement. |
| **External review required?** | Yes — before first EU engagement |

## TIER 3 — Decline for Now (External Legal Counsel Required)

No client engagements in these jurisdictions without prior external legal review by a qualified local attorney.

### 🇩🇪 Germany
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Laws** | GDPR (strictest enforcement in EU), TTDSG (Telecommunications-Telemedia Data Protection Act), German AI regulations |
| **Status** | 🔴 Decline — External legal counsel required before any engagement |
| **Requirements** | Qualified German/DSA attorney review mandatory; DPA must meet German-specific requirements; TTDSG adds additional consent requirements for cookies and electronic communications |
| **Consent standard** | TTDSG requires explicit consent for certain data processing activities beyond GDPR baseline. German DPAs are particularly strict on enforcement. |
| **Risk level** | Very High |
| **Notes** | Germany has the most aggressive GDPR enforcement record in the EU (e.g., Hamburg DPA fines Meta, DSK actions against Google). This is not a jurisdiction to enter without a German-qualified attorney. Decline until external counsel is engaged. |
| **External review required?** | Yes — mandatory before any Germany engagement |

### Other EU Member States with Strict National Overlay
Jurisdictions like France (CNIL enforcement), Ireland (DPC — significant Big Tech enforcement history), and the Netherlands (AP enforcement) are not Tier 3 strictly, but each has national authorities that actively enforce. EU client engagements should be treated as Tier 2 with external review.

## RED LINES — Absolute

The following rules are non-negotiable regardless of jurisdiction:

1. **No EU/UK client served without a fully executed DPA** — DPA must be signed before any work begins.
2. **No German client served without external legal counsel** — German legal review is mandatory.
3. **No special category data** (health, biometric, financial, racial/ethnic origin, political opinions, trade union membership, genetic, sexual orientation) processed without explicit additional approval and separate addendum.
4. **No automated decisions** affecting individuals without human review — all significant outputs reviewed by qualified personnel.
5. **No outreach** on behalf of any client without documented consent evidence on file (per Consent Chain Framework).
6. **No jurisdiction not listed above** without CLA assessment and CEO approval before engagement.
