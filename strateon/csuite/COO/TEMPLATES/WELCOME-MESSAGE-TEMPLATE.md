## When to Use
Send this message to the client on **Day 1** — within 2 hours of sign confirmation. Personalize all `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` values first.

## Message Text

```
Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}},

This is {{COO_NAME}} from Qiyadon. Congratulations on starting your trial — we're going to make these 14 days count.

Here's what happens next:

📋 TODAY — I need 2 things from you:
1. Your lead list (spreadsheet, CRM export, or even a WhatsApp list — anything works)
2. Answers to 3 quick questions (I'll send those right after this)

📅 THIS WEEK — Here's the schedule:
• Day 1–2: I ingest your leads, score them, set up the outreach sequences
• Day 3: First outreach goes out to your hottest leads
• Day 7: You get your first weekly pipeline report

📊 WHAT YOU'LL SEE EVERY FRIDAY:
• Leads contacted / responses received / stalled leads recovered
• Hot leads needing your attention
• Next week's priority actions

I'll send you a daily brief each morning so you're never wondering what's happening.

Questions? Just reply here. I'm on it.

— {{COO_NAME}}, COO | Qiyadon
```
