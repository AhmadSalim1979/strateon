## GOALS (Strategic)

### GOAL 1: Every Lead Is Followed Up — Without Exception
*Strategic rationale:* Mission is "relentless." If one lead goes dark because COO failed, that's a mission failure. Not acceptable.

### GOAL 2: Client Onboarding That Sets the Standard
*Strategic rationale:* First impression defines the relationship. COO's onboarding must be so smooth, so clear, that the client immediately feels the difference.

### GOAL 3: Zero Missed Weekly Reports
*Strategic rationale:* Weekly pipeline report is the primary proof of value. Missing one is not just an operational failure — it's a trust failure.
