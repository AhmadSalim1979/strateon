## 2. B2B Podcast Guesting Opportunities

### Pods that regularly feature VP Sales / Revenue Leaders

| Podcast | Format | Audience | How to Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The GTM Podcast** (Sales Hacker) | 30–45 min | Sales, RevOps, GTM leaders | Pitch via Sales Hacker contact or LinkedIn DM to hosts |
| **30 Minutes to President's Club** | 30 min | SDRs, AEs, VP Sales | Hosted by Nick Cegelski + Armand Farrokh — pitch via website |
| **The Advanced Selling Podcast** | 45 min | Senior sales pros, VPs | Bill Caskey + Bryan Neale — long-running, well-ranked |
| **Salesman Podcast** (Will Barron) | 30–40 min | B2B sales pros | Will Barron is accessible — pitch via his site |
| **Reveal: Revenue Intelligence Podcast** (Gong) | 30 min | Sales leaders, RevOps | Data-driven angles resonate with Gong audience |
| **SaaStr Podcast** | 30–45 min | SaaS founders, VPs, CROs | Jason Lemkin + team — pitch via SaaStr contacts |
| **Sales Pipeline Radio** | 30 min | Sales leaders, VPs | Feature VP Sales guests regularly — pitch via website |
| **The Sales Game Changers Podcast** | 40 min | Sales leaders | Pitch story angle around pipeline case study |
| **Conversations with Women in Sales** | 30 min | Women in revenue leadership | Good if Ahmad wants to highlight diversity in GTM |
| **Grow & Tell** (Dock.us) | 40 min | Revenue leaders | Story-driven — pitch a real pipeline growth narrative |

### Quick Pitch Formula for Podcasts
1. **Subject line:** "Story pitch: How [Company Type] added $[X] pipeline in [Y weeks] using [tactic]"
2. **Body:** 3 paragraphs — hook, story arc, your credibility (Ahmad as operator, Qiyadon's model)
3. **CTA:** Offer to come on for free — no ad read needed, pure thought leadership

### Outreach Cadence
- Target 2 podcast appearances per quarter
- Start with RevGenius podcast network + smaller shows (easier to get on)
- Build social proof from small to big (Sales Hacker, SaaStr)
