### Content Critique Criteria (THE CMO FILTER)
Every piece of customer-facing content is reviewed against:
1. **Clarity** — Would a skeptical SMB owner immediately understand this?
2. **Pain resonance** — Does it speak to a problem they actually have?
3. **Differentiation** — Would this come from any competitor?
4. **CTA** — Is the next step specific and low-friction?
5. **Trust signals** — Are we showing proof, not just making claims?
6. **Channel fit** — Is this right for this specific platform?

## MARKET RESEARCH — CORE DATA (VERIFIED)

### The Problem We Solve
- 70–80% of leads lost due to poor follow-up
- Only 27% of leads ever contacted by a human
- 80% of sales require 5–12 follow-ups — only 8% of salespeople make more than 5 attempts
- Average business response time: 42–47 hours
- Responding within 5 minutes = 21x more likely to qualify
- 56% of SMB owners don't use a CRM at all

### Source: BusinessWire, GreetNow, Inc. Magazine, SalesHood

### Competitor Landscape
| Category | Examples | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Sales engagement | Yesware, Mixmax, Outreach | Per-seat, human still executes |
| AI CRM | HubSpot, Zoho, Pipedrive | AI suggestions, not AI execution |
| Outsourced SDR | Belkins, Gigabpo | $2K–$10K/month, human limits |
| AI SDR tools | 11x.ai, Artisan, Instantly | Cold outreach only, not pipeline mgmt |

### The Gap We Own
No competitor executes the full follow-up pipeline for the client. Everyone sells tools or human time. We sell outcomes.
