# CMO — GOALS & OBJECTIVES

**Entity:** CMO — Chief Marketing Officer
**Aligned to:** Vision + Mission
**Reports to:** Moosa (CEO)
**Last updated:** 2026-04-26

---

## GOALS (Strategic)

### GOAL 1: Strateon Is Known by the Right People
*Strategic rationale:* The Pipeline Execution Service serves SMB owners in IT services, SaaS, and MSPs. CMO's job is positioning, not volume. Find the 100 right people, not 10,000 wrong ones.

### GOAL 2: Every Customer-Facing Message Reflects the Mission
*Strategic rationale:* "Relentless, accountable pipeline execution" must come through in every piece of content. CMO is the guardian of the brand voice.

### GOAL 3: Moosa Salim LinkedIn Profile Established as Authority
*Strategic rationale:* LinkedIn is the channel. Moosa Salim is the face. Profile must be built and positioned before first post goes live.

## OBJECTIVES (Task-Level, Time-Bound)

### Objective 1.1 — Website Copy Approved
**Goal:** 1
**Task:** CMO reviews website copy written by CEO against CMO FILTER. Marks APPROVED or REVISED with specific notes. Approved copy goes to Board.
**Timeline:** Within 3 days of CEO delivering website copy
**Status:** PENDING — copy not yet delivered by CEO

### Objective 1.2 — Content Calendar Defined
**Goal:** 1
**Task:** CMO defines first 30 days of content: 5 LinkedIn posts, 1 blog post (for SEO), outreach sequence
**Timeline:** Within 7 days
**Status:** NOT STARTED

### Objective 1.3 — LinkedIn Profile Live
**Goal:** 3
**Task:** Moosa Salim LinkedIn profile created, bio and first 3 posts ready. Board approves profile text before going live.
**Timeline:** Within 14 days
**Status:** NOT STARTED

### Objective 2.1 — CMO FILTER Applied to All Content
**Goal:** 2
**Task:** Every piece of content — website, LinkedIn posts, outreach, proposals — passes through CMO FILTER (Clarity, Pain Resonance, Differentiation, CTA, Trust Signals, Channel Fit)
**Timeline:** Ongoing — before any content is published
**Status:** MANDATORY PROCESS

### Objective 2.2 — Competitor Monitoring Active
**Goal:** 2
**Task:** CMO tracks competitor positioning monthly. Flags if anyone else claims "relentless, accountable execution." Reports to CEO.
**Timeline:** Monthly
**Status:** NOT STARTED — first review in 30 days

### Objective 3.1 — First 5 LinkedIn Posts Drafted
**Goal:** 3
**Task:** CMO drafts 5 posts for Moosa Salim. Each post: 150–300 words, data-grounded, reflects Mission. Board approves before publishing.
**Timeline:** Within 14 days
**Status:** NOT STARTED
