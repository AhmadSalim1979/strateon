### DESCRIPTION

The Strateon icon is a simplified, geometric mark derived from the brand's visual identity. It is a standalone symbol — **NOT** the full wordmark — used when space is limited or at small display sizes.

**Design concept (V3):** A stylized, angular "S" constructed from two overlapping chevron-like shapes, pointing upward-right to suggest momentum and progress. Rendered in Electric Blue (`#2563EB`) on a Navy (`#0A1628`) square background with rounded corners (12px radius), or reversed.

---

### USAGE RULES

**✅ DO:**
- Use as favicon in browser tabs and bookmarks.
- Use as social media profile images (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook page).
- Use as app icons on iOS and Android home screens.
- Use in any context where the full wordmark would be too small to read.

**❌ DO NOT:**
- Use the icon as a substitute for the full horizontal logo in presentations, letterhead, or business cards.
- Add effects: gradients, shadows, glows, or 3D extrusions.
- Change the background color from the approved Navy or reversed Off-White.
- Recreate the icon with different geometry — only the approved V3 file may be used.
- Use at sizes below 16px (illegible).
