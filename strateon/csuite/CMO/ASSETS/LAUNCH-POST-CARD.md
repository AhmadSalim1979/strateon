### DESCRIPTION

A square-format social media card (1080×1080px / 1:1 aspect ratio) designed for launch announcements. Features the Strateon logo, a bold headline announcing the launch, and the tagline. Designed for Instagram feed, LinkedIn feed, and Twitter feed.

**Layout:**
- Background: Navy (`#0A1628`)
- Headline: Large, white, Inter ExtraBold — e.g., "STRATEON IS LIVE"
- Sub-headline: Electric Blue accent line
- Logo: Horizontal lockup, centered or lower-third positioned
- Tagline: Small white text
