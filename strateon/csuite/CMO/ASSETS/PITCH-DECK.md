### DESCRIPTION

The cover slide for the Strateon pitch deck. This slide is used for all investor-facing presentations, partner briefings, and board updates. It sets the tone for the entire deck.

**Design:**
- Background: Solid Navy (`#0A1628`)
- Centered horizontal logo lockup (white version)
- Tagline beneath logo: "Where Strategy Meets Scale."
- No photos, no imagery — logo and tagline only

### USAGE RULES

**✅ DO:**
- Use as the first slide of every investor-facing pitch deck.
- Use as a meeting header slide for board updates.
- Keep the design static — no entrance animations on the cover.

**❌ DO NOT:**
- Add a photo or illustration to the cover — it dilutes the brand.
- Change the tagline without CMO and CEO approval.
- Use the Launch Post Card design as a pitch deck cover — wrong context.
- Add additional text beyond the tagline.
