### DESCRIPTION

The Strateon primary logo is a horizontal lockup consisting of:
1. **Icon mark** — A stylized geometric mark (S-form or abstract arrow/arrowhead) rendered in Electric Blue (`#2563EB`) on Navy (`#0A1628`) or as a reversed mark on light backgrounds.
2. **Wordmark** — "STRATEON" in Inter ExtraBold (800 weight), set in Navy (`#0A1628`) on light backgrounds, or White (`#FFFFFF`) on dark backgrounds.
3. **Tagline (optional)** — "strateon.com" or a product tagline in Inter Regular (400) beneath the wordmark in Slate (`#64748B`).

### USAGE RULES

**✅ DO:**
- Use on solid Navy (`#0A1628`) or White (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds only.
- Maintain 1:1 clear space ratio on all sides.
- Use the reversed (white) version on dark photo backgrounds when the navy version lacks contrast.
- Scale proportionally — never resize width/height independently.

**❌ DO NOT:**
- Stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo.
- Add drop shadows, gradients, or effects to the logo.
- Place the logo on busy photographic backgrounds without an overlay box.
- Use the logo at any size below the minimum width.
- Change the color of any element of the logo.
- Place the wordmark above the icon (vertical lockup is not approved).
- Add underlines, animated effects, or color transitions.
