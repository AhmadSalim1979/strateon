## 2. PAIN SECTION — Why Your Pipeline Is Leaking

### Section Header
Your Leads Are Going Dark. Not Because You Don't Care. Because You're Busy.

### Intro Copy
The leads aren't cold because you don't care. They're cold because human bandwidth runs out. The follow-up that should happen Tuesday at 10am becomes Friday at 4pm — and by then, the lead is gone. For founder-led SMBs, this is the silent revenue killer: qualified prospects who raised their hand and never heard back.

### Pain Point 1 — Follow-Up Inconsistency
**You had every intention of following up.**
Between closing deals, running ops, and keeping clients happy, the follow-up that was supposed to go out Tuesday morning quietly slips to Friday afternoon — if it goes out at all. Your pipeline is full of leads that went dark not because they lost interest, but because you ran out of hours in the day.

### Pain Point 2 — The Owner Is Too Busy to Chase
**Closing is a full-time job. Following up is a second one.**
You're a founder. Your job is to close. But right now you're also trying to be an SDR, a marketer, and an account manager — all at once. The leads stack up. The follow-ups get shorter. The response rates drop. That's not a motivation problem. That's a capacity problem.

### Pain Point 3 — Your CRM Is a Graveyard
**Half your pipeline is dormant — and you know it.**
Your CRM is full of leads that went dark weeks ago. Some of them were hot. Some of them asked for pricing twice and then disappeared. You didn't follow up because you got busy. They moved on. Now your pipeline looks healthy but your close rate says otherwise.

### Pain Point 4 — Warm Leads Go Cold
**The longer the silence, the harder it is to reconnect.**
A lead that goes unanswered for 72 hours is 5x harder to re-engage than a lead that gets a response in the first hour. Every day of silence costs you ground. By the time you're back in front of them, they've already talked to someone else — or decided to do nothing.

### Closing Line
You're not the problem. Your system is.
No founder can be relentless across every lead, every day, while also doing their real job: closing deals. That's why we built Strateon — to handle the follow-up that used to fall through the cracks.
