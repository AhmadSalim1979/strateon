Before generating, understand what the logo must communicate:

- **Who we are:** An AI-led institution. Not a startup. Not a SaaS app. An institution with a real AI CEO (Moosa) and a human Board.
- **What we do:** Pipeline execution — relentlessly, accountably.
- **Who we serve:** SMB owners in IT services, SaaS, and MSPs — skeptical, pragmatic, decision-makers who have been burned by tools and agencies before.
- **What we must signal:** Trust. Authority. Intelligence. Execution. Not playfulness. Not startup energy. Not "techy" in a generic way.

## COLOR PSYCHOLOGY — WHY THESE COLORS

### The Problem with Generic Tech Logos

90% of B2B SaaS logos use the same blue gradient. It says nothing except "we are a software company." Strateon must stand apart — immediately — through its visual identity.

### Chosen Palette

**Primary: Deep Slate Navy — #0F172A**
- Meaning: Authority, depth, institutional weight. Not generic tech blue — this is the color of serious organizations.
- Why it works: Dark enough to command respect. Cool enough to signal intelligence without screaming "AI startup."
- Usage: Main wordmark text, icon background, dark section backgrounds.

**Accent: Deep Teal — #0D9488**
- Meaning: Growth, forward motion, trust. Different enough from generic blue that it stands out without being loud.
- Why it works: Teal bridges the gap between conservative (navy) and progressive (tech). It says "we're serious AND we're moving."
- Usage: Icon accent, CTA buttons, single graphic elements, the "pipeline" line.

**Secondary: Warm Off-White — #F8F9FA**
- Meaning: Clarity, openness, breathing room.
- Usage: Background on light sections, light variant wordmark.

**Dark Text: #1E293B**
- For body text on light backgrounds. Never pure black — #000000 is too harsh for B2B.

**DO NOT USE:**
- Bright blue (#3B82F6 or similar) — this is the most overused color in tech
- Purple or pink gradients — signals the wrong era and wrong audience
- Neon green — too "startup" and too loud for this brand
- Orange as a primary — too casual, too e-commerce
- Full black — too harsh, no sophistication

## LOGO DESIGN PRINCIPLES

### What Separates Professional from Generic

| Generic Logo | Professional Logo |
|---|---|
| Gradient blue blob | Flat, intentional color shapes |
| Robot or brain icon | Abstract geometry with meaning |
| Comic or rounded font | Clean, geometric sans-serif |
| Multiple colors competing | One primary, one accent, one neutral |
| Symmetrical and predictable | Asymmetrical with intentional tension |
| Looks like 100 other logos | Immediately distinctive |

### The Pipeline Element

"Pipeline" must be communicated subtly — not literally. Think:
- **Converging lines** that suggest flow without showing a physical pipe
- **Connected nodes** that suggest a system (a pipeline) without being a diagram
- **A directional arrow or flow line** integrated into the icon
- The idea of momentum and direction, not static objects

Do NOT use: literal pipe shapes, water/fluid imagery, cartoon tubes.

### The AI Element

AI must be suggested, never depicted. Think:
- **Geometric abstraction** — a neural network simplified to 4–6 nodes with clean connecting lines
- **A circuit line** — a circuit trace that forms part of the icon's shape
- **A single node with radiating lines** — suggesting intelligence, processing, connection

Do NOT use: humanoid robots, brains, lightbulbs, neural network diagrams, "AI" text anywhere on the logo.

### Typography Guidelines

**Font Style:** Geometric sans-serif — clean, flat, serious.

**Approved directions (choose one):**
1. Custom geometric letterforms for "STRATEON" — each letter slightly modified to suggest connection/flow (the "S" could have a subtle line through it, the "O"s could be nodes)
2. Inter Bold or DM Sans Bold as the base — clean and modern without being Inter-generic
3. A custom wordmark where letters are connected by a thin horizontal line (the "pipeline" of text)

**What to AVOID in typography:**
- Script fonts (cursive, handwriting) — says "boutique," not "institution"
- Serif fonts — too traditional, wrong era
- Rounded/friendly fonts — too casual
- All-lowercase — too startup, not enough authority
- Letter-spacing that is too loose — loses the institutional weight

**Capitalization:** Title Case: "Strateon" — not "STRATEON" (all caps is shouting), not "strateon" (lowercase is too casual)

## PRIMARY LOGO — FULL SPECIFICATION

### Composition

**Layout A (Horizontal — preferred for website header and documents):**
```
[ICON]  STRATEON
        [Tagline — optional, small beneath]
```

- Icon: Left-aligned, approximately 48x48px at 1x resolution
- Wordmark: Right of icon, vertically centered on the icon
- Spacing between icon and wordmark: 12–16px
- Tagline (if used): Below wordmark, small, in #64748B

**Layout B (Stacked — for square placements, social media profile):**
```
   [ICON]
 STRATEON
 [Tagline]
```

### Icon Design — The Strateon Mark

**Concept:** A geometric abstraction of both a pipeline (flow, convergence) and AI (nodes, connections).

**The shape:**
- A hexagonal or angular container (suggests structure, institution, precision — NOT a circle, which is too common)
- Inside the hexagon: 4–6 small circles (nodes) connected by lines in a converging pattern
- One or two lines break outside the hexagon boundary, suggesting flow beyond — the pipeline
- OR alternatively: The hexagon IS formed by connected nodes — so the shape IS the network

**Color treatment:**
- The icon container: Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A)
- The nodes and connecting lines inside: Deep Teal (#0D9488)
- The "exit line" (the flow beyond the shape): Teal, slightly thicker, suggesting direction

**What it should NOT look like:**
- A brain
- A robot face
- A lightbulb
- A speech bubble
- A generic gradient blob
- A gear or wrench

### Wordmark

- Font: Custom or heavily modified geometric sans-serif
- Weight: Bold (700)
- Letter-spacing: Tight (-0.02em to -0.04em)
- Color: Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A) on light backgrounds
- Color: White (#FFFFFF) on dark backgrounds

## ICON-ONLY VERSION — FOR FAVICONS, APP ICONS, SMALL PLACEMENTS

**The icon must work standalone at sizes as small as 32x32px.**

**Design:**
- Simplified hexagon shape (the container)
- 3 nodes visible (not 6 — at small sizes, fewer is clearer)
- One converging line
- In single-color: use Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A) ONLY
- The icon must be recognizable as "Strateon" even without the wordmark

**Do NOT include:**
- Text at any size below 64x64px
- The tagline anywhere
- Multiple colors in the icon-only version

---

## MONOCHROME VERSION — FOR PRINT, ONE-COLOR APPLICATIONS

**When to use:** Embossing, single-color print, embroidery, stamps.

**Design:**
- Icon + wordmark in a SINGLE color
- Color: Deep Slate Navy (#0F172A) OR reverse (white on navy)
- No teal accent in monochrome — the navy IS the brand color
- The icon nodes should be slightly smaller (to maintain weight balance without the teal accent)

- Wordmark: flat deep navy (#0F172A)

TYPOGRAPHY:
- "STRATEON" in bold geometric sans-serif (like Inter Bold or DM Sans Bold)

STYLE:

- NO cartoons, NO robots, NO brains, NO lightbulbs, NO gears

ASPECT RATIO: Wide format for horizontal layout (icon left, wordmark right)

## REVISION NOTES — IF GENERATION NEEDS ADJUSTMENT

If the first generation doesn't match the brief, here is what to request in subsequent prompts:

**Problem: Generated a gradient or 3D logo**
→ Add to prompt: "STRICTLY FLAT DESIGN. Zero gradients. Zero shadows. Zero 3D. Vector aesthetic only."

**Problem: Icon looks like a brain or neural network diagram**
→ Add to prompt: "The nodes must be few (4 maximum), large, and geometric — not a detailed neural network. Think: 4 dots connected by lines. Abstract, not scientific."

**Problem: It looks like a generic tech startup logo**
→ Add to prompt: "The design must feel institutional and serious, like a consulting firm or financial institution that also happens to be tech-forward. NOT a Silicon Valley startup app."

**Problem: The wordmark looks like Inter or Arial**
→ The prompt will generate something close. For production, the wordmark should be custom typeset using a font designer. The AI generation is a direction-finder, not final production art.

**Problem: Too playful or too rounded**
→ Add to prompt: "Angular, sharp, geometric. The opposite of rounded or friendly. This is an institution, not a consumer app."

## FINAL PRODUCTION NOTE

AI image generation is excellent for exploring directions quickly and finding an initial concept that resonates. However, the final Strateon logo — for use across all brand materials — should be refined by a professional graphic designer or produced as vector art in Figma, Illustrator, or Affinity.

The prompts above will yield a strong starting concept. The designer then cleans up the geometry, perfects the letterforms, and produces the production-ready SVG and PNG files in all required formats and sizes.

**Deliverables to request from the designer:**
- Primary logo (horizontal): SVG + PNG @1x, @2x, @3x
- Icon-only: SVG + PNG @1x, @2x, @3x
- Monochrome (light background): SVG + PNG
- Monochrome reverse (dark background): SVG + PNG
- Favicon: 16x16, 32x32, 48x48 (ICO + PNG)
- Social media profile image: 400x400 PNG
- Color breakdown: Pantone, HEX, RGB, CMYK values for all brand colors
