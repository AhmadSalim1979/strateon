**Title:** Your Pipeline Has a Handoff Problem

**One-liner:** Deals don't die in the pipeline. They die at the handoff — from one stage to the next, one team to the next, one tool to the next.

**ICLE:** Pipeline execution breakdown at stage transitions — handoff gap

**Core argument:** Standard pipeline tools track stages but not transitions. Deals are dropped at handoff points — not because of bad intent, but because context doesn't transfer and follow-up sequences don't continue. The structural fix is running a function at every transition, not just moving deals between stages.
