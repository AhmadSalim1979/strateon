**POST-021 — Your Pipeline Has a Handoff Problem**

*One-liner:* Deals don't die in the pipeline. They die at the handoff — from one stage to the next, one team to the next, one tool to the next.

**ICLE:** Pipeline execution breakdown at stage transitions — handoff gap
**Status:** READY FOR AHMAD — publish on LinkedIn

---

Every deal goes through at least three transitions before it closes:

Marketing-qualified lead to sales-qualified opportunity.
First call to proposal.
Proposal to signed contract.

Each transition is a choke point. Each choke point is where deals go dark.

**What standard pipeline tools miss:**

A CRM tracks stages. It tells you which deal moved from Stage 2 to Stage 3. It does not tell you what happened during the transition — whether context was handed off, whether the follow-up sequence ran, whether anyone was assigned to the new stage.

The move happened. The function didn't.

This is why pipeline visibility is an illusion. You can see every deal at every stage and still have no idea which ones will actually close — because the gaps between stages are where execution breaks down, and standard tools don't measure those gaps.

**What the data shows:**

Research across B2B pipeline performance consistently shows that deal velocity drops sharply at each transition point — not because the deal lost momentum, but because the handoff failed. The rep in Stage 3 doesn't have what the rep in Stage 2 had. The context is lost. The follow-up window opens with no action.

The deal didn't stall. It was dropped.

**The structural fix:**

Pipeline execution isn't about moving deals from stage to stage. It's about running the function at every transition — making sure context transfers, sequence continues, and someone is assigned before the window opens.

Qiyadon runs that function. From intent signal to closed won, every transition has an owner and a sequence. Silence is escalated, not ignored. Handoffs are clean, not assumed.

Your pipeline isn't broken. It's being dropped at every handoff.

What's your biggest handoff gap right now?
