## LinkedIn Post (POST-014)

---

Your pipeline died in the last 24 hours.

You just don't know it yet.

Here's what the data actually says about the first day after a lead enters your system:

Buyers who respond to outbound outreach within the first hour are 3x more likely to convert than those contacted 24+ hours later.

That's not a CRM insight. That's not a lead quality problem. That's a speed problem — and it's entirely execution.

**The 24-hour window.**

Between the moment a lead lands in your system and the moment your first follow-up lands in theirs, there's a window where intent is still warm. After that window closes, response rates drop. Not gradually — measurably.

Most SMB pipelines don't fail at day 30. They fail at hour 2.

**What this looks like in practice:**

You ran a campaign. Leads came in. Your team is busy. By the time someone picks up the first lead, it's been 18 hours. The email goes out. No reply. Two more days. Silence. Dead deal.

Nobody made a bad decision. Nobody was negligent. The system just wasn't fast enough.

**The problem is invisible because it happens before you notice it.**

You see the leads that responded. You don't see the ones that would have responded if you'd gotten there on time.

This is why "we follow up" is not a pipeline strategy. "We follow up when we have bandwidth" is how silent revenue leaks happen.

Qiyadon runs the execution layer — initial outreach within 24 hours, systematic, every lead, every day. Every Friday, you get a clear picture of where your pipeline actually stands.

Not a dashboard. Not a tool. The function.
