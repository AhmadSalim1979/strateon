# Post 002 — Pipeline Mechanics Problem

**One-liner:** If you're a founder and you've had a "quiet month" more than twice — that's not a lead generation problem. That's a pipeline leak.

---

Most B2B founders I talk to at the $1M–$3M mark have the same story:

"We have leads. We're growing. But the revenue is lumpy — some months great, others quiet, and I can't quite figure out why."

They assume it's a lead generation problem.

It's almost never a lead generation problem.

It's almost always a pipeline mechanics problem — the gap between "someone filled out a form" and "someone signed a contract" has leaks you're not systematically tracking.

The firms that have solved this aren't doing anything magical. They've just made the implicit explicit: what's actually happening between your first touch and your close? Where do deals go quiet? What does your follow-up cadence actually look like vs. what you think it looks like?

If you're a founder at an MSP, SaaS, or professional services firm and you've had a "quiet month" more than twice in the last year — that's not a sign you need more leads. That's a sign your pipeline has a leak.

What's one leak you've noticed in your own pipeline? I've been mapping these for a while — genuinely curious what's showing up for others at this stage.
