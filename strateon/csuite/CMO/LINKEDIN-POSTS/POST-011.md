# Post 011 — The SMB Stacking Problem

**One-liner:** MSPs, SaaS founders, and professional services owners aren't missing tools. They're missing the system to run the tools they already have.

**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Status:** Published — 2026-05-10 (with image) — confirmed by Ahmad
**Created:** 2026-05-08

---

We keep seeing the same pattern in founder-led businesses between $500K and $5M ARR.

The stack looks like this:
- A CRM they set up two years ago and never finished
- A LinkedIn Sales Navigator subscription they use occasionally
- An email outreach tool they paid for but barely configured
- A calendar they share with their whole team
- A spreadsheet somewhere that has "the real numbers"

No single person is accountable for the system. The founder is running it, but they're also running everything else. The tools exist. The data exists. What's missing is the function.

This is what we call "stacking" — and it's different from "not having tools." Stacking happens when you have everything you need in theory but can't get it to function as a system in practice.

The businesses that broke out of stacking didn't buy a new tool. They didn't hire an operations consultant. They found someone who could own the execution of the pipeline — the daily follow-up, the stage management, the escalation — as a function.

Not a tool. Not a dashboard. Not a one-time setup.

A function.

Does your pipeline have a function — or just a stack?
