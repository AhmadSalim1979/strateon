# Post 004 — Systems Problem Disguised as Motivation

**One-liner:** "I follow up three times, then I feel like I'm annoying them, so I stop." — every founder. The fix is a system, not a pep talk.

---

I spent the last few weeks talking to founders — MSPs, SaaS shops, professional services firms — mostly in the $500K–$3M range, mostly founder-led.

Here's what came up most:

They're not struggling to get leads. They have leads. They're struggling to convert them without it feeling like a second job.

Specific patterns I heard:
- "I follow up three times, then I feel like I'm annoying them, so I stop"
- "I don't know if the silence means they're not interested or just busy"
- "We hired someone to run our marketing and it was... fine. But not pipeline fine."

Every single one of these is a systems problem disguised as a motivation or effort problem.

If you're a founder at this stage and this sounds familiar — I'd genuinely like to compare notes. What's your pipeline situation looking like right now?
