# Post 003 — Enterprise Tool, Startup Team

**One-liner:** A founder with 5 employees and a well-tracked pipeline will outrun a 30-person company with a shiny CRM nobody updates.

---

Here's a conversation I have at least once a week:

Founder: "We need to get serious about our pipeline."
Consultant/vendor: "You need a CRM."
Founder: "How much?"
Consultant/vendor: "Well, enterprise is $30K setup, plus $2K/month, but there's a smaller plan for $800/month."
Founder: "...we have 8 people."

The problem isn't that CRMs are bad.

The problem is that most small B2B firms are being sold infrastructure for where they're going, not for where they are.

At $300/month, you don't need a $50K implementation. You need someone to look at the 8-step process you already have, figure out where deals are actually dying, and fix that specific thing.

A founder with 5 employees and a well-tracked pipeline will outrun a 30-person company with a shiny CRM nobody updates.

Has anyone else run into this — the "enterprise tool, startup team" mismatch? Genuinely curious how others have navigated it.

**Visual concept:** A small team of 5 people around a laptop, doing real work, with a green upward arrow showing momentum. Opposite side: a massive enterprise CRM dashboard with 47 tabs open, a confused face icon, and a red "underutilized" label. Text: "Right-size the tool. Scale the execution."
