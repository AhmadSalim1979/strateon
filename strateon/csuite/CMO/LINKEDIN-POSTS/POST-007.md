# Post 007 — The Follow-Up Gap

**One-liner:** 92% of B2B reps give up after four follow-ups. That's where your pipeline dies.

---

Most B2B deals don't die because the product fails.

They die because the follow-up stops.

The data is clear: 80% of B2B deals require five or more touch points to close. Yet 92% of salespeople abandon a prospect after four attempts or fewer. That means the majority of your pipeline is going dark — not because the lead is unqualified, but because the follow-up ran out of steam.

For founder-led SMBs, this is structural, not behavioral. The founder is wearing seven hats. Following up every single lead methodically isn't a priority — it's a luxury. So the pipeline stalls, the deals go cold, and the revenue that was within reach just... disappears.

**The real cost isn't the lost close.**

It's the lost signal. When a deal goes dark without a proper close-out, you don't learn why. You don't know if the timing was wrong, the champion left, the budget got frozen, or the need shifted. You just have silence.

The firms that run healthy pipelines don't necessarily have better leads or better products. They have a system that treats silence as a trigger — not a verdict.

Something that forces action when a deal hasn't moved in 10 days. Someone who escalates the quiet instead of waiting for the prospect to come back.

**That's the difference between a pipeline that decays and a pipeline that compounds.**

How many deals quietly died in your pipeline last month — not from competition, just from silence?
