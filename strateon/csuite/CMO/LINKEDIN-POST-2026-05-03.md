## Post Text

**You have a leads problem. You know it. Your CRM proves it.**

Every week, a list goes out. Every week, half the people on it never get a first touch. The ones who did reply? Somewhere between "I'll get back to you" and completely forgotten.

This isn't a discipline problem. It's a bandwidth problem.

Founder-led SMBs don't have a sales team. They have the founder — who's also doing ops, support, product, and everything else. Following up every single lead well takes time they don't have. So it doesn't happen. And the leads rot.

Qiyadon is a pipeline execution service. We don't generate leads for you. We make sure the ones you already have don't go dark.

Here's what that means in practice:
- Every new lead gets a touch within 24 hours
- Unanswered outreach gets escalated — we don't let silence sit
- You get a weekly pipeline report every Friday

Not a dashboard you have to check. Not a reminder you have to act on. We run the follow-up sequence, we push the urgency, we close the loop.

If you're a founder running ads, running events, building a list — and you know you're leaving money on the table because you can't keep up with the follow-up — that's who we're built for.

**How many leads did you generate last month that went completely cold?**

Drop it in the comments. 👇

## Suggested Visual / Reel Concept

**Visual direction:** Clean, minimal, slightly raw — not polished agency creative.

**Image concept:** A split screen. Left side: a phone screen showing a long list of leads with "Last contacted: 2 weeks ago" annotations. Right side: the same list, but now marked with green checkmarks, reply timestamps, and "Followed up ✓" labels. Simple text overlay. Dark background, white text.

**Reel concept (30–60 sec):** Founder sits at desk, opens laptop,眉头皱起 as they scroll through a long lead list. Voiceover: "How many of these are you actually following up on?" Cut to them receiving a Friday report on WhatsApp — pipeline cleaned, leads actioned. Founder's face shifts from overwhelm to relief. Text on screen: "No lead left behind. Every Friday reported." Link in bio.

**Why this works:** Speaks directly to the pain point without embellishment. The visual contrast (chaos → control) is immediately recognizable to any founder who's stared at a stagnant CRM.
