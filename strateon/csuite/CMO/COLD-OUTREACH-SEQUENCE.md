## Personalization Notes

### Before Sending Each Email:

1. **Verify their name + title** — Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator or Apollo for accuracy
2. **Reference something specific** — Recent fundraise, product launch, LinkedIn post, hiring news
3. **Customize the calendar link** — Replace with Ahmad's actual Calendly / Cal.com link
4. **Customize the social proof number** — Use the "Case Study Post" as the anchor for the $X in pipeline figure
5. **Avoid if they're a current customer** — Check against any existing Qiyadon clients first

### Segmentation by Company Stage:

| Stage | Email Tone Adjustment |
|---|---|
| **Series A** | Emphasize "build it right" — early sales motion, lean team, no extra headcount |
| **Series B** | Emphasize "scale without chaos" — pipeline complexity, multiple reps, forecasting |
| **Series C** | Emphasize "predictability" — revenue consistency, board pressure, RevOps alignment |

### Tracking & Metrics to Monitor:

- **Open rate** — If below 40%, test subject line variants
- **Reply rate** — If above 8%, your personalization is working
- **Meeting booked rate** — Target: 2–3% of total list per sequence
- **Pause and iterate** after first 50 emails — adjust subject lines and personalization depth
