## TOP 8 PLATFORMS

### 1. LinkedIn Articles
- **URL:** https://www.linkedin.com/start/home
- **Access:** Sign in with existing LinkedIn profile (free). Go to "Write an article" from your profile.
- **Content type:** Thought leadership, business insights, industry opinions, company news, case studies
- **Audience:** B2B professionals, founders, decision-makers, MSP owners, tech buyers

---

### 2. Medium
- **URL:** https://medium.com
- **Access:** Sign up free with Google or email. Immediate access to publishing.
- **Content type:** Long-form essays, how-tos, opinions, deep dives, storytelling around Qiyadon
- **Audience:** Tech-savvy readers, startup community, developers, entrepreneurs, global

---

### 3. Substack
- **URL:** https://substack.com
- **Access:** Sign up free → create your publication → start writing. Email list auto-built.
- **Content type:** Newsletter-style blogs, essays, behind-the-scenes, business updates, long-form
- **Audience:** Subscribers who opt-in — engaged, loyal readers; great for building an owned email list

---

### 4. Ghost
- **URL:** https://ghost.org
- **Access:** Free self-hosted option → download → deploy to your own domain. Or use Ghost(Pro) paid.
- **Content type:** Clean, minimalist blogging — editorials, news, product updates, long-form content
- **Audience:** Your own website visitors; great for brand-owned content without platform dependency

---

### 5. Reddit — r/entrepreneur / r/smallbusiness / r/MSP
- **URL:** https://www.reddit.com/r/entrepreneur | https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness | https://www.reddit.com/r/MSP
- **Access:** Free Reddit account → join subreddits → post content or engage in discussions
- **Content type:** Value-add posts, lessons learned, business stories, occasional product mentions (no spam)
- **Audience:** Entrepreneurs, small business owners, MSP operators — community-driven, advice-seeking

---

### 6. Indie Hackers
- **URL:** https://www.indiehackers.com
- **Access:** Free sign up → go to "Posts" section → publish your blog/update
- **Content type:** Startup journey, revenue updates, product launches, lessons, behind-the-scenes of Qiyadon
- **Audience:** Founders, indie hackers, early-stage startup builders — highly engaged startup community

---

### 7. Product Hunt
- **URL:** https://www.producthunt.com
- **Access:** Free maker account → submit product launches or updates
- **Content type:** Product launches, major feature updates, milestones, launches — not daily blogs per se but key for visibility
- **Audience:** Builders, early adopters, investors, tech press

---

### 8. Dev.to
- **URL:** https://dev.to
- **Access:** Free sign up with GitHub/Google/Twitter → start publishing immediately
- **Content type:** Technical blogs, developer-facing content, coding lessons, tool reviews, tech stack behind Qiyadon
- **Audience:** Developers, tech founders, DevOps, engineers — great for B2D (developer-driven growth)
