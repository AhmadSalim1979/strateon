## WHAT WE'RE REPLACING

### Current Launch Post Card (Asset 7C — ORIGINAL BRIEF VERSION)

The current Launch Post Card is described in the original CMO brand asset brief as follows:

**Layout & Composition:**
- Clean, modern card format optimized for social media and digital distribution
- Bold headline area at the top
- Core value proposition in the center
- CTA button area at the bottom

**Text Elements (PROBLEM):**
- Large "NOW LIVE" banner/badge layered prominently in the design
- Headline text
- Supporting copy
- CTA text

**Visual Style:**
- Qiyadon brand colors (professional, modern, trust-inducing palette)
- Clean typography hierarchy
- Minimal illustration or graphic element
- Card-based format with clear visual zones

**The Issue:**
The "NOW LIVE" text sits on the base layer — it is not conditionally timed or gated. It is always visible. This makes the card feel urgent, busy, and time-bound in a way that undermines the evergreen credibility we're building. The card should be able to stand alone at any moment in time without screaming for attention.

---

## BOARD SUMMARY

**Why this change:**

The "NOW LIVE" element was a mistake in the base layer design. It makes every card feel like it expires the moment it's posted. We want assets that hold their value — cards that can be posted today, next month, or next quarter and still feel relevant.

**What's different:**
- Removed the "NOW LIVE" urgency badge from the base design
- All other elements remain: layout, composition, style, brand treatment
- The card is now a clean, evergreen launch announcement

**What's the same:**
- Everything else. This is a targeted fix, not a redesign.
