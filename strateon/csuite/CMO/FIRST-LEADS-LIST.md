## How to Use This List

1. **Verify on LinkedIn** — Search "[Company] VP Sales" or "[Company] Head of Sales" before outreach
2. **Personalize each email** — Reference something public about their company's recent product, funding, or growth
3. **Trial-first prospects** — Lead with the free 14-day trial offer in Email 1 PS
4. **Paid conversation prospects** — Lead with the Pipeline Health Check, include trial PS as optional lower-friction path
5. **Start with 5** — Don't blast all 10 at once. Pilot with 3–5, track replies, iterate
6. **Prioritize fit over prestige** — Series A/B companies often respond faster and convert better for Pipeline-as-a-Service
