## 1. BRAND PALETTE ALIGNMENT

**Board's stated palette:** Navy `#0B1F3A`, Teal `#00C2CB`, Gold `#F5A623`

| Element | Expected | Reported | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Background | White `#F4F7FA` | White | ✅ Match |
| Primary accent | Teal `#00C2CB` | Teal `#00C2CB` | ✅ Match |
| Letterform color | Navy `#0B1F3A` or Teal | Teal | ⚠️ See note |

**CMO Note:** The letterform is teal per the Board's description. This is acceptable — teal on white is a strong, recognizable brand execution and is consistent with Image 2 (Primary Horizontal Logo), which uses teal as the dominant accent on white. However, **navy on white** is also canonical (per Image 6 rejection context). The Board should clarify: does the "S" render in navy or teal? Both are valid brand executions, but they create different impressions — teal reads as more distinctive and tech-forward; navy reads as more authoritative and institutional.

**Recommendation:** Either is brand-compliant. Teal is preferred for standalone marks; navy is preferred for wordmark integrations. The pipeline cut-outs are the key identifier — the fill color is secondary.

## 2. LEGIBILITY AT SMALL SIZES

| Size | Concern | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| 16×16 | Three cut-outs may blur to a solid block | ⚠️ RISKY — requires explicit testing |
| 32×32 | Cut-outs should hold if strokes are thick enough | ✅ Likely pass |
| 64×64 | Full detail visible | ✅ Pass |

**CMO Concern:** The three horizontal cut-outs are the defining brand feature, but at 16×16 they risk collapsing into visual noise — a muddy gray blob instead of a recognizable "S." This is the same concern raised in Image 7 (Icon/Favicon) review.

**Required test:** Before approving, this asset must be tested at actual 16×16, 32×32, and 64×64 pixel sizes. If the cut-outs become unreadable, the "S" must be thickened or simplified to two cut-outs at small sizes.

**Passing criteria:** The "S" must be identifiable as an "S" and not a plain hexagon or solid block at 16×16.

## 4. VERDICT

**RECOMMENDATION: APPROVED WITH REQUIRED SCALE TESTING**

The asset is directionally correct and brand-compliant. Before final approval for production use, the following must be confirmed:

### Required Actions Before Production
