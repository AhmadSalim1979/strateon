## 1. TRIAL STRUCTURE OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Duration & Name
- **Name:** Pipeline Free Trial (14 Days)
- **Duration:** 14 calendar days from go-live date
- **Go-live date:** First outreach message sent to a lead (not the signup date)
- **No extensions.** A trial that expires must be treated as an opportunity to close; do not extend. If special circumstances arise, CEO approval required.

### 1.2 Goal
Convert qualified trial users into paying Starter or Growth clients at Day 14 using a specific, irresistible conversion offer — not a generic upsell.

---

## 2. ELIGIBILITY — WHO QUALIFIES

**Not everyone gets a trial.** Qiyadon qualifies trial applicants to protect against:
- Time wasters who sign up to compare tools
- Companies with no real pipeline intent
- Users who will never generate revenue

### 2.1 Mandatory Requirements (ALL must be met)
1. **Direct application submitted** — Prospect completes the application form at qiyadon.com/pipeline-leak-audit, selects "Trial" as inquiry type, and submits a minimum of 20 leads. No discovery call required.
2. **ICP fit confirmed** — Role is VP Sales, Head of Sales, Director of Sales Ops, or Founder/CEO doing sales. Company is B2B SaaS, $2M–$20M ARR, 20–200 employees.
3. **Minimum 20 leads submitted** — Trial user must submit at least 20 real leads within 3 business days of trial start. This is a commitment signal.
4. **CRM access confirmed** — Either Qiyadon has received CRM export, or WhatsApp/email lead forwarding is actively configured. A trial with no leads to follow is meaningless.
5. **Jurisdiction confirmed** — Client has confirmed their territory so Qiyadon can ensure outreach compliance before Day 1.

### 2.2 Disqualifications
Trial access is NOT available to:
- Companies with fewer than 5 active leads
- Founders doing outbound cold prospecting (not a fit — Qiyadon follows up, it doesn't generate)
- Anyone who has previously had a Qiyadon trial (one trial per company, ever)
- Regulated industries (legal, financial services, healthcare) that require CLA review and sign-off — these go straight to paid onboarding
- Companies outside SaaS (consultancies, agencies, service businesses)

### 2.3 Screening Process
- Application submitted via qiyadon.com/pipeline-leak-audit with "Trial" inquiry type selected
- Minimum 20 leads must be included with the application
- If disqualified, Qiyadon tells them why and offers a direct paid path or alternative (e.g., audit-only service)
- Trial access is not guaranteed even if the prospect requests it — "apply" framing is intentional

---

## 2.4 HOW TO APPLY

The prospect follows these steps to apply for a free trial:

1. **Fill out the form** at [qiyadon.com/pipeline-leak-audit](https://qiyadon.com/pipeline-leak-audit)
2. **Select "Trial"** as the inquiry type
3. **Submit a minimum of 20 leads** with the application — these serve as the commitment signal
4. **COO reviews** the application and, if qualified, **activates the trial within 24 hours**

Trial begins on the day the COO activates it. First outreach goes out within 2 hours of lead ingestion on Day 1.

## 3. WHAT'S INCLUDED DURING TRIAL

### 3.1 Lead Cap
| Prospect's Assessed Tier | Trial Lead Cap | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Likely Starter | **25 leads** | Half of Starter tier. Enough to feel value, not enough to be complete. |
| Likely Growth | **50 leads** | Capped at Starter ceiling to create upgrade friction and demonstrate value. |

Leads are hard-capped. If prospect submits more than the cap, excess leads are queued for after-trial (if converted) or returned/deleted (if not). No overages during trial.

### 3.2 Follow-Up Channels
- **WhatsApp only** — Primary channel for all outreach, follow-ups, and check-ins
- Email channel is **NOT included** during trial (this creates a clear difference from paid tiers and keeps the trial simple)
- If WhatsApp delivery fails, Qiyadon flags via internal log — no automatic fallback to email without explicit upgrade

### 3.3 Cadence & Features
- **3-touch follow-up sequence** per lead (same as Starter)
- **Lead scoring** — Hot / Warm / Cold / Dormant applied to all trial leads
- **Dormant lead recovery** — Leads inactive 7+ days trigger a recovery sequence (same as Starter)
- **First outreach within 2 hours** of lead submission (Starter SLA)
- **Response tracking** — All lead replies tracked and actioned

### 3.4 Reporting During Trial
- **No Friday report during trial** — Instead, a **Day 7 Check-In** is sent on the 7th day (mid-trial)
- **Day 7 Check-In format:**
  ```
  📊 TRIAL CHECK-IN — Day 7 of 14
  How's it going?
  • Leads contacted: [X]/[Y]
  • Replies received: [X]
  • Leads reactivated: [X]
  • Hot leads: [X]
  • Needs your decision: [X]
  Reply with any questions — happy to adjust approach.
  — Moosa, Qiyadon
  ```
- **Day 14 Final Report** — Sent on the final day (see Section 5)

## 5. DAY 14 — CONVERSION OFFER

### 5.1 The Outcome Window

Day 14 is not the end — it is the **conversion moment**. The prospect receives:

**A) The Final Trial Report (Day 14 message):**
```
📊 YOUR TRIAL REPORT — Day 14 of 14
Here's what happened over the last 14 days:

• Leads managed: [X]
• First outreach sent within 2 hours: [X]%
• Replies received: [X]
• Leads reactivated from dormant: [X]
• Hot leads identified: [X]
• Decisions needed from you: [X] (and their outcomes)

📈 Your pipeline health improved by [X]% this week.

Your trial ends today. Here's what happens next 👇
```

**B) The Conversion Offer (sent same day, after the report):**
```
🎯 YOUR TRIAL CONVERSION OFFER — Valid 72 hours

You've seen what Qiyadon can do. Here's what it costs to keep going:

**[ASSESSED TIER] — $[X]/month**

[What's included — bullet list matching the tier]
[What's included — bullet list]

This is your current rate locked in for 12 months:
✅ [Full tier inclusions]
✅ [X] active leads
✅ [Lead ingestion SLA]
✅ Weekly Friday report
✅ [Strategy call if Growth/Scale]

❌ Offer expires in 72 hours.
❌ After today, standard pricing applies.
❌ No trial extension available.

To activate: Reply YES or book a 10-min call to finalize.

— Moosa, Qiyadon
```

### 5.2 Conversion Pricing

The trial conversion offer is **tier-specific with a loyalty discount**:

| Assessed Tier | Normal Price | Trial Conversion Price | Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | $300/mo | **$250/mo** | 12-month annual |
| **Growth** | $750/mo | **$600/mo** | 12-month annual |

**Annual commitment is required** at these rates. Month-to-month is not available at the trial conversion price — that is reserved for post-trial new customers who pay full price.

**Lock rationale:** If a prospect took a 14-day trial and still won't commit to 12 months, they're not the right customer. We don't need to incentivize further. Full price for month-to-month is our filtering mechanism.

### 5.3 What Happens at 72 Hours (No Response)
- After 72 hours with no reply, Qiyadon sends **one final message**:
  ```
  Just checking in — your trial conversion offer expires in [X] hours.
  If this isn't the right time, I completely understand.
  You can still come back at standard pricing anytime.
  No pressure — just wanted to make sure you had the option.
  — Moosa
  ```
- After that final message, **no further outreach** for 60 days
- If they resurface after 60 days, they go through discovery again and are offered standard pricing (no trial, no loyalty discount)

## 6. SUCCESS CRITERIA — WHEN TO OFFER CONVERSION

### 6.1 Metrics That Trigger a Conversion Offer

Qiyadon does not offer conversion to every trial user. Conversion offers go only to prospects who meet **at least 3 of 5** of the following criteria:

| # | Metric | Threshold | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lead response rate | ≥ 80% of leads replied to at least once | Shows the pipeline is real and leads engage |
| 2 | Dormant reactivation rate | ≥ 15% of dormant leads reactivated | Proves Moosa creates recovery value |
| 3 | Lead volume submitted | ≥ 20 real leads submitted during trial | Indicates the client has enough pipeline to manage |
| 4 | Engagement with Day 7 check-in | Client replied to or acknowledged the Day 7 check-in | Shows they're paying attention and will read Friday reports |
| 5 | Escalation decisions made | Client made at least 1 decision on an escalated lead | Shows they trust Moosa enough to act on its recommendations |

**If the prospect meets ≥ 3 of 5:** Conversion offer goes out on Day 14.

**If the prospect meets < 3 of 5:** Day 14 message is a **closing message, not a conversion offer**:

```
📊 YOUR TRIAL — FINAL DAY

14 days is up. Here's what we saw:
• Leads submitted: [X]
• Replies received: [X]
• Reactivated: [X]

It looks like [X months] might not have been enough time to see the full picture — or the pipeline volume wasn't there to really put the system to work.

If you'd like to try again in 3 months when your pipeline is more active, you're welcome to reach out.
If you'd like to chat about a different approach, I'm here.
No hard feelings either way.

— Moosa, Qiyadon
```

**Principle:** Do not offer paid conversion to a prospect who couldn't demonstrate value with a free trial. Their failure now doesn't mean they're a bad fit forever — it means the timing is wrong. Close the door politely now so you can reopen it later.

### 6.2 Success Definition for Qiyadon

Qiyadon defines a successful trial as one that **converts to a paid client by Day 17** (Day 14 + 72 hours). Trials that don't convert are not failures — they're qualification exercises.

Target metrics:
- **Trial-to-paid conversion rate: ≥ 40%** — If below 40%, the trial is too easy to get or the offer is too weak
- **Trial-to-paid conversion rate: ≤ 70%** — If above 70%, we're being too restrictive or underpricing the trial

Reviewed quarterly by CPO + CEO.

## 7. TRIAL OPERATIONS

### 7.1 Onboarding SLA During Trial

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 | Application submitted with minimum 20 leads. Contract signed (even for trial, a service agreement is required). DPA signed. |
| Day 1 | COO reviews application. If qualified, trial is activated within 24 hours of application. Client's leads are ingested, scored, and queued within 2 hours of activation. |
| Day 2 | Moosa confirms lead count. If over cap, excess leads are queued for post-trial. |
| Day 2–3 | Outreach begins on all submitted leads within 2 hours of ingestion. |
| Day 7 | Day 7 check-in sent. Client replies or doesn't. Logged. |
| Day 14 | Final report + conversion offer sent. |
| Day 17 | If no response: final follow-up. Trial officially closed. |

### 7.2 Data & Lead Handling After Trial

**If prospect converts to paid:**
- All trial leads transition seamlessly into paid pipeline
- No re-ingestion required; existing scoring and sequences carry over
- Lead cap increases to tier limit; previously queued leads are now activated

**If prospect does NOT convert:**
- Client data (lead list, scores, sequence history) is retained for 30 days
- After 30 days: all client data deleted per DPA
- No follow-up emails after Day 17 final message unless the prospect reaches out
- If prospect resurfaces within 60 days: offer standard pricing (no trial, no loyalty discount)

### 7.3 Abuse Prevention

To prevent trial abuse (signing up repeatedly without intent):
- **One trial per company, per lifetime** — Not one trial per person, one per company entity
- CRM email domain is recorded at signup and cross-checked against prior trial database
- Lead volume minimum (20 leads) is enforced — no leads submitted within 3 business days = trial is suspended and client is notified
- Direct application required — no self-serve trial signup without application
