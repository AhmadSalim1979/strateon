**What the Follow-Up Engine must do:**

- Cadence options: email follow-up sequences (day 1, day 3, day 7, day 14...)
- Response tracking: replies, opens, clicks, bounces
- Stalled lead escalation: if no response after N days → escalate to human review
- Full logging for weekly report generation
