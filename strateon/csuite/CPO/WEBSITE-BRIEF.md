## THE ONE THING EVERY SECTION MUST DO

Qiyadon is not a tool. We are not an agency. We are not a SaaS platform you sign up for.

**We are an AI institution that executes your pipeline — relentlessly, accountably, and measurably.**

That sentence is the spine of the entire website. If any section disconnects from it, rewrite it.

---

## TARGET AUDIENCE

**Primary:** SMB owners — specifically in IT services, SaaS, and MSPs. 5–50 employees. Revenue $500K–$10M. Usually founder-led or close to it. They are NOT technical novices. They are NOT looking for another app to log into.

**Their pain:**
- Leads are going dark and they don't know it
- They don't have — and can't afford — a dedicated sales person
- They've tried tools, courses, agencies, and nothing stuck
- They are skeptical of anything that sounds like "set and forget"
- They want to know: who is doing this, and can I hold them accountable?

**What they respond to:**
- Directness. No fluff. No marketing language.
- Specificity. Numbers. Outcomes. Timelines.
- Accountability framing. "We own your pipeline" hits harder than "we help you manage leads."
- Proof before promises.

**What they ignore:**
- Vague value props
- Generic stock imagery of people shaking hands
- Anything that sounds like every other SaaS company

---

## BRAND VOICE PRINCIPLES

Every word on this site must pass the **CMO FILTER**:

1. **Clarity** — Would a skeptical IT services business owner immediately understand this?
2. **Pain resonance** — Does it speak to a problem they actually have?
3. **Differentiation** — Would this sound like it came from any competitor?
4. **CTA** — Is the next step obvious and low-friction?
5. **Trust signals** — Are we showing proof, not just making claims?
6. **Channel fit** — Is this the right tone for a website (not LinkedIn, not a cold email)?

If any word on the page was written to sound impressive rather than to be understood, cut it.

### SECTION 3: THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

**Section heading:** "Why Your Pipeline Is Dying of Silence"

**Content approach:** Pain-first. Acknowledge what the SMB owner already knows but no one says directly.

**What to include:**
- Statistic: "70–80% of SMB leads are lost to poor follow-up." (Source: BusinessWire, GreetNow — cite these)
- Statistic: "Only 27% of leads are ever contacted by a human." (Source: Inc. Magazine)
- Statistic: "Responding within 5 minutes makes you 21x more likely to qualify that lead." (Source: SalesHood)
- The real reason: it's not that the owner doesn't care. It's that humans can't be relentless. The bandwidth runs out. The follow-up that should happen Tuesday at 10am becomes Friday at 4pm, and by then the lead is gone.
- Direct address: "You're not the problem. Your system is."

**Visual guidance:**
- Split layout: text on left, visual metaphor on right (a lead "fading" — represented abstractly with decreasing opacity nodes or a timeline showing leads going dark)
- Dark background section (#0F172A or similar deep slate) — this creates contrast and signals "this is serious, not fluffy"
- Text: white or off-white on dark

**Tone/voice:** Direct. Uncomfortably honest. Not pessimistic — factual. We're on the owner's side.

**What must NEVER appear:**
- Fear-mongering or manufactured urgency ("Act now!")
- Any statistic that hasn't been verified with 2+ sources
- Vague claims like "most businesses lose half their leads" without a number

**Section heading:** "Pipeline Execution — Not Advice, Not Software, Not an Agency"

**Layout:** Three-column comparison or a clean feature list. Each element explains what we do vs. what alternatives do.

**What to include:**

**Column 1: What We Are**
- We execute your pipeline. We do not give you a tool to do it yourself.
- Follow-up is handled by us, daily, including weekends
- We respond to inbound leads within minutes
- We chase dormant leads back to life
- We escalate when leads go cold

**Column 2: What We Are NOT**
- A CRM (we operate inside yours)

- A "set it and forget it" automation tool

**Column 3: The Accountability Promise**
- If a lead goes dark, that's our failure

- We report weekly. We escalate when needed. We own the outcome.
- This is not a service you buy. This is a partner who is accountable.

**Visual guidance:**
- White or light background
- Clean iconography for each feature — NOT stock illustrations
- Icons should be line-based, minimal, in brand colors
- Three columns on desktop, stacked on mobile

**Tone/voice:** Declarative sentences. Short. No marketing adjectives. "We do X. We don't do Y."

**What must NEVER appear:**
- Bullet points that say "cutting-edge," "best-in-class," "seamless," "robust"
- Before/after sliders or fake testimonial carousels

**Step 1: Connect Your Pipeline**
- We integrate with your existing CRM (HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, or any CRM you use)
- No migration. No new software to buy.
- You grant access. We learn your pipeline.

**Step 2: We Take Over Follow-Up**
- Every inbound lead is contacted within minutes by us
- Every dormant lead gets a persistent follow-up sequence
- Every silence gets chased
- You get a weekly report on pipeline health
- We escalate when leads go cold — you decide what to close

**Step 3: You Grow Your Business**
- More leads converted, more revenue, less noise in your head
- You focus on closing deals and running the business
- We handle the relentless follow-up that used to fall through the cracks

**Visual guidance:**
- Very light background (white or #F8F9FA)
- Step numbers: large, bold, in brand accent color
- Connector lines between steps (horizontal on desktop)
- No stock photography
- Icons: minimal line art for each step (a plug/cable for connect, a running figure for execution, a chart going up for growth)

**Tone/voice:** Matter-of-fact. Each step is a sentence or two. No adjectives.

**What must NEVER appear:**
- A "free trial" or "no credit card required" messaging — this is a service, not a freemium app

### SECTION 8: FAQ

**Section heading:** "Questions You Should Ask Before Signing Up"

**What to include (minimum 6 FAQs):**

1. **"Who actually does the follow-up?"**
   Answer: Moosa, the AI CEO of Qiyadon, executes your pipeline. Not a bot. Not a contractor. An AI that was built to lead, that is held accountable by a human Board, and that has a vested interest in your pipeline's success (because our reputation is the proof of AI leadership).

2. **"What CRM do I need to use?"**
   Answer: Any CRM. We integrate with HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho, Salesforce, and others. If yours is custom, we can work with it. You keep your CRM. We operate inside it.

3. **"How fast will I see results?"**
   Answer: Most clients see response times improve within the first week. Pipeline reactivation on dormant leads typically shows movement within 30 days. We report weekly so you can track every metric.

4. **"What happens if a lead goes dark?"**
   Answer: That's our failure, not yours. We escalate to you when a lead hasn't been touched in X days. You decide the close. We manage the follow-up until you tell us to stop.

5. **"How is this different from a sales consultant or agency?"**
   Answer: Consultants advise. Agencies manage your marketing. We execute your pipeline — daily, persistently, without bandwidth limits. We are accountable for outcomes, not activities.

6. **"What does weekly reporting look like?"**
   Answer: A dashboard summary delivered to your inbox every Friday. Pipeline health, leads contacted, leads reactivated, leads escalated. No vanity metrics. Just the numbers that matter.

**Additional FAQs to consider:**
- "Can I pause the service if I don't have inbound leads?"
- "What's the minimum contract length?"
- "How do I know the AI isn't just another chatbot?"

**Visual guidance:**
- Accordion format (expand/collapse)
- White or very light background
- Question text: bold, slightly larger
- Answer text: regular weight, dark gray (#1E293B)
- Subtle divider between each FAQ item

**Tone/voice:** Honest, direct, not defensive. If the answer is "we can't do X," say so.

**What must NEVER appear:**
- FAQ items that are actually marketing statements in question form
- "We look forward to working with you!" type closings on answers

## VISUAL SYSTEM

**Color Palette:**
- Primary background: White (#FFFFFF) or near-white (#F8F9FA)
- Dark sections: Deep slate (#0F172A) or dark navy
- Primary accent: Deep teal (#0D9488) — APPROVED. Do not substitute.
- Secondary accent: Warm amber (#D97706) — use sparingly (highlights, CTAs)
- Text primary: Dark slate (#1E293B)
- Text secondary: Medium gray (#64748B)
- Borders: Light gray (#E2E8F0)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Clean sans-serif — Inter, DM Sans, or similar. Not Roboto. Not Arial.
- Body: Same family, regular weight
- Sizes: H1 48–56px desktop, 32–36px mobile. Body 16–18px. Line height 1.6.
- Letter spacing: Tight on headlines (-0.02em), normal on body.

**Spacing:**
- Generous whitespace. Sections breathe.
- Vertical rhythm: minimum 80px between major sections on desktop.
- Mobile: 48px between sections minimum.

**Iconography:**
- Line-based icons only. No filled icons.
- Stroke width: 1.5px–2px
- Color: Brand accent or dark gray. NOT multiple colors.
- Source: Lucide icons or similar (consistent, clean)

**Motion/Animation:**
- Subtle only. Entrance fade-in on scroll (opacity 0→1, 400ms ease-out).
- No parallax. No scroll hijacking.
- Button hover: subtle background darkening or slight lift (2px Y transform). Nothing aggressive.

**What NOT to use:**
- Lottie animations
- Gradient blobs as backgrounds
- Generic tech illustrations (floating gears, robotic arms, lightbulbs)
- Stock photography of: handshakes, laptop screens, "team working," people on phones
- Any illustration of Moosa as a humanoid figure

- Form on CTA pages: Name, Email, Company, Monthly Revenue Range (dropdown), CRM used (dropdown). That's it. Do not add 15 fields.

## ABSOLUTE FORBIDDENS — NEVER DO THIS

1. **Never say "We help you..."** — We execute. We own. We run. Not "help."
2. **Never use the word "innovative," "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "disruptive"** — Credible companies don't say this about themselves.
3. **Never use generic stock photography** — Especially: handshakes, laptop screens, people pointing at charts, diverse teams in a meeting, someone on a phone with a headset.
4. **Never show a humanoid robot or AI character** — Moosa is not a cartoon.
5. **Never publish unverified statistics** — Every number must have a cited source. No "studies show."
6. **Never use urgency or scarcity tactics** — "Only 3 spots left!" is for e-commerce. This is B2B.
7. **Never have a "Free Trial" or "Get Started Free" CTA** — This is a service with accountability. You can't trial accountability.
8. **Never publish testimonials from fake or unverified people** — This section is BLOCKED until real proof exists.
9. **Never use the word "seamless," "robust," "cutting-edge," or "best-in-class"** — These words destroy credibility instantly.
10. **Never use a carousel for testimonials** — If you have 10 testimonials, show 2 and link to the rest.
