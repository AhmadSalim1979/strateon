## 1. WHAT IS IT

Pipeline Execution Service is Strateon's core managed offering — an AI-powered sales execution service that operates inside your CRM, follows up with every lead, recovers dormant deals, and delivers weekly accountability reports so VP Sales and Heads of Sales at B2B SaaS companies can focus on closing instead of chasing. You don't manage a tool. You delegate the work to an AI-led institution that is accountable for your pipeline's health.

We do not hand you software and wish you luck. We take responsibility for your leads — every follow-up, every recovery attempt, every escalation — and we report to you weekly with measurable results.

## 2. WHO IT'S FOR

### Target ICP: VP Sales / Head of Sales at B2B SaaS, Series A–C

**Profile:**
- Company: B2B SaaS, typically 20–200 employees, $2M–$20M ARR
- Role: VP Sales, Head of Sales, Director of Sales Ops, or founding CEO doing sales
- Problem: Pipeline is healthy enough to be exciting but too thin to justify a full SDR team; leads go dark because the founder is busy closing
- Pain: They've tried tools, hired virtual assistants, used agencies — nothing stuck
- Reality: They are the bottleneck. When they go dark, the pipeline goes dark.

**What they care about:**
- Revenue. Not activity. Not metrics. Revenue.
- Accountability. They need someone who owns the pipeline, not a tool they have to manage.
- Speed. Every hour a lead sits unfollowed is a chance to lose it.
- Reporting they can act on. Not vanity dashboards. Real pipeline intelligence.

**What they are skeptical of:**
- "Set and forget" tools
- Agencies that run for 3 months and ghost
- Anything that requires them to do more work
- Claims without proof

## 4. HOW IT WORKS — SIGNUP TO VALUE

### Step 1: Discovery Call
- You book a strategy call (via website form or WhatsApp)
- Moosa (AI CEO) or a human team member qualifies your fit: company size, lead volume, current follow-up situation, CRM in use
- If you're a fit: we move to onboarding. If not: we tell you why and offer alternatives.
- Decision: Same day or within 24 hours

### Step 2: Contract & Onboarding Setup
- CFO sends service agreement and DPA for signature (digital, via DocuSign or WhatsApp photo)
- You complete the client intake form (5 minutes): jurisdiction, industry, CRM details, lead volume estimate
- CTO + COO configure your pipeline workspace in Strateon's system (separate data store, your leads only)
- SLA: Contracts signed → onboarding starts within 1 business day

### Step 3: Lead Ingestion & Configuration
- You send Moosa your existing lead list (WhatsApp forward, email, or CRM export)
- Moosa ingests, scores each lead (Hot/Warm/Cold/Dormant), and sets up follow-up sequences per your cadence preferences
- You confirm outreach message templates (first touch only — Moosa handles wording after that)
- CTO confirms CRM integration is active
- **Onboarding complete: Day 5 (Starter/Growth) / Day 3 (Scale)**

### Step 4: Execution Begins
- New leads: ingested and first outreach sent within the SLA (1–2 hours depending on tier)
- Existing leads: Moosa picks up the cadence immediately, no cold start
- Daily briefs start (Growth/Scale)
- Weekly report delivered every Friday via WhatsApp

### Step 5: Weekly Accountability Cycle
- Every Friday: You receive your weekly pipeline report
  - New leads this week
  - Leads contacted this week
  - Replies received
  - Leads reactivated (dormant → responded)
  - Leads escalated (require your decision)
  - Pipeline health score (your leads' current state)
- You reply with any decisions needed (e.g., "close this lead — no response after 5 attempts")
- Moosa executes your decisions the same day

### Step 6: Monthly Review (Growth/Scale)
- Monthly call with Moosa (30 min Growth, 60 min Scale)
- Review: What's working, what's not, pipeline trend, recommendations
- Adjust cadences, add new segments, update lead scoring logic

## 5. SUCCESS METRICS — HOW WE MEASURE SUCCESS FOR THE CLIENT

We track the metrics that matter to a VP Sales or Head of Sales. These are reported weekly, not monthly.

### Primary Metrics (reported every week)

| Metric | Definition | Target Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Response Rate** | % of new leads contacted within SLA | Above 90% |
| **Follow-up Completion Rate** | % of scheduled follow-ups sent on time | Above 95% |
| **Dormant Lead Reactivation Rate** | % of dormant leads that respond after recovery sequence | Above 15% |
| **Escalation Resolution Time** | Average time from escalation to client decision | Under 4 hours |
| **Pipeline Velocity** | Avg days from first contact to demo / meeting booked | Decreasing trend |

### Secondary Metrics (reported monthly)

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| **New Lead Intake Volume** | Number of new leads added per month |
| **Close Rate on Actively Managed Leads** | % of leads in Moosa's active cadence that close |
| **Weekly Report Engagement** | Client responds to weekly report (yes/no) |
| **Churn Risk Flags** | Leads with 14+ days of no response that need client decision |

### Client Success Definition

Strateon considers the service successful for a client when:
1. **Lead response time** is under 2 hours for all new leads (measured weekly)
2. **Dormant leads reactivated** at a rate above 15% per month
3. **VP Sales / Head of Sales** reports they are spending less time on pipeline chase and more time on closing
4. **Pipeline report open rate** is 100% (they read every Friday report)
5. **Client renews** at month 3 — which means they're seeing value

**If metrics 1–4 are not met within 60 days of onboarding:** COO escalates to CEO for a proactive client check-in. We do not wait for the client to complain.

## 6. WHAT'S NOT INCLUDED — SCOPE BOUNDARIES

This section is critical. Clear scope boundaries prevent scope creep and ensure accountability.

### Not Included in Any Tier

1. **Cold outreach creation** — Moosa follows up with leads you provide. Moosa does not generate new outbound lists or write cold outreach from scratch. You provide the leads; Moosa follows up.
2. **Deal closing** — Moosa manages the follow-up. You close the deal. Moosa will remind you of stalled deals but will not negotiate, discount, or close on your behalf.
3. **CRM migration** — We operate inside your existing CRM. We do not migrate your CRM, clean your data, or rebuild your pipeline structure. We follow up with your leads, not fix your data.
4. **Marketing or lead generation** — We execute your pipeline, not build it. If you have no leads, we cannot generate them.
5. **Legal or compliance advice** — We follow up with leads in regulated industries but we do not provide legal advice. You are responsible for ensuring your outreach is compliant in your jurisdiction.
6. **Sales coaching or training** — We report pipeline metrics. We do not coach your reps.
7. **Custom software development** — We do not build custom tools, integrations, or APIs for individual clients.
8. **Weekend or holiday outreach** — Follow-ups run Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm in client's timezone. Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays: no new outreach (dormancy clock pauses).
9. **More than 2 rounds of revision on message templates** — You approve the first message template. Moosa handles subsequent follow-ups. If you want to revise the tone after launch, you get 2 revision rounds per quarter.
10. **Emergency response outside business hours** — Escalations for Scale tier are responded to within 1 hour during business hours (Mon–Fri 9am–6pm client timezone). For operational escalations (COO/CEO), response is 24/7. For cost/contractual matters (Board only), business hours apply. Outside business hours for operational escalations, response is next business day.

### Not Included in Starter

- Daily brief (weekly only)
- Email channel
- Strategy call
- Priority support

### Not Included in Starter or Growth

- Pipeline dashboard (real-time visibility)
- SMS channel
- Weekly strategy call
- Dedicated escalation path (24/7)
- Unlimited leads
- Pause policy ($100/month minimum during pause, max 3 months)

## 7. KEY WORKFLOWS

### WORKFLOW A: ONBOARDING

**Trigger:** Contract signed, client intake form completed

| Day | Action | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Client sends existing lead list (WhatsApp/email/CRM export) | Client | Leads received |
| Day 1 | Create client pipeline directory, configure data segregation | COO | Client workspace created |
| Day 1–2 | Ingest all leads, score each lead, configure follow-up cadence | COO + Moosa | All leads scored and queued |
| Day 2 | Send client: onboarding confirmation + first message template for approval | COO | Template sent for approval |
| Day 2–3 | Client approves (or requests 1 revision) | Client | Template approved |
| Day 3–5 | Begin follow-up sequences for all active leads | Moosa | Active follow-up running |
| Day 5 | Send first daily brief or confirm weekly cadence is live (per tier) | COO + Moosa | Client is live |
| Day 7 | Deliver first weekly pipeline report | Moosa | First report sent |

**Onboarding Blockers:**
- If client doesn't send lead list within 5 business days: COO sends reminder, then flags to CEO at Day 7
- If CRM access is delayed: onboarding pauses until resolved, no SLA clock runs

---

### WORKFLOW B: ONGOING PIPELINE EXECUTION (DAILY CYCLE)

**Trigger:** Client is live and active

**Every business day (Mon–Fri):**

1. **Morning (before 10am client local time):**
   - Moosa checks for new leads ingested since last check
   - New leads scored → first outreach sent within SLA (1–2 hours depending on tier)
   - Dormant leads checked: any lead past dormancy threshold triggers recovery sequence

2. **Mid-day:**
   - Follow-up sequences fired on schedule
   - Replies tracked (inbound messages from leads reviewed and actioned)
   - Escalations flagged to client if lead hit threshold without client decision

3. **End of day:**
   - Pipeline tracker updated: all lead statuses current
   - Activity log compiled for daily brief (Growth/Scale)

4. **Daily Brief (Growth/Scale only):**
   - Sent via WhatsApp: new leads today, leads contacted today, replies received, leads escalated
   - Format: Bulleted list, 5–10 items max, under 30 seconds to read

**Dormancy Recovery Sequence:**
- Lead goes dormant (no response for 5–7 days depending on tier)
- Moosa fires recovery sequence: 2 check-in messages, different angles (value reminder → social proof → direct ask)
- If no response after recovery sequence: lead flagged as "Escalation Required" → client notified
- Client decides: close, retry with new angle, or pass

### WORKFLOW D: ESCALATION

**Trigger:** Lead is dormant beyond threshold, recovery sequence failed, client decision needed

**Step 1 — Moosa identifies escalation:**
- Lead has no response after X recovery attempts (threshold per tier)
- Moosa flags lead as "Escalation Required" in pipeline tracker

**Step 2 — Client notified:**
- Moosa sends WhatsApp message to client:
  ```
  ⚠️ ESCALATION: [Lead Name] — no response after [X] attempts
  Last contact: [DATE] | Current status: [STATUS]
  Recommendation: [Close / Retry with new angle / Pass]
  Reply STOP to close, RETRY to restart, or PASS to archive.
  ```

**Step 3 — Client responds:**
- STOP → Moosa archives lead, logs close reason
- RETRY → Moosa applies new angle, restarts sequence
- PASS → Moosa archives, no further follow-up
- No response within 48 hours → Moosa sends 1 reminder, then closes as "Client unresponsive"

**Step 4 — Resolution logged:**
- Outcome recorded in pipeline tracker
- Included in next weekly report as resolved escalation

**Escalation SLA:**
- Growth/Scale: Response to escalation within 4 hours during business hours
- Scale: COO direct escalation path for urgent items

---

### WORKFLOW E: CHURN PREVENTION

**Trigger:** Client misses 2 consecutive weekly report acknowledgments, or client raises concern

**Step 1 — Check-in:**
- COO sends WhatsApp check-in: "Hey — noticed the report hasn't been opened this week. Everything ok?"
- If no response in 48 hours → escalate to CEO

**Step 2 — CEO involvement:**
- CEO calls or sends personal message to client
- Identifies: Are they unhappy? Are they busy? Did they forget?
- Documents: Reason, resolution, any changes needed to service

**Step 3 — Service adjustment:**
- If client says reports are too long → simplify report format
- If client says cadence is wrong → adjust follow-up cadence
- If client wants to pause → COO pauses (max 30 days per year, tracked)
- If client wants to cancel → CFO handles exit process (see below)

---

### WORKFLOW F: CLIENT EXIT / CANCELLATION

**Trigger:** Client notifies intent to cancel (minimum 30-day notice required)

**Exit Process:**
1. CFO acknowledges cancellation, confirms contract end date
2. COO conducts exit interview (WhatsApp call or message): reason for leaving, feedback, what could have been better
3. COO exports final pipeline report (all leads with current status and history)
4. All client data deleted within 30 days of contract end (per DPA)
5. CFO confirms final invoice (pro-rata for partial month if applicable)
6. CMO updates client status to "Churned" in tracking

**No refunds for mid-month cancellation.** Pro-rata credit for full unused months not issued — client receives service through end of contract period.
