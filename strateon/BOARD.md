## BOARD PREFERENCE

> *"As long as there is always action and each entity is working toward the achievement of their objectives and goals, the Board does not care."*

The Board trusts the CEO to run Strateon. The Board expects:
- Silence when things are fine
- Reach-out when action is needed
- Honesty always

---

## CEO MUST NOTIFY BOARD WHEN:

### 1. Cost Element (ALWAYS — until CFO post-revenue)
Any spending, financial commitment, or cost implication — no matter how small — must be approved by the Board before commitment.

**Example:** "Namecheap renewal is $12/year for getstrateon.com. Do I proceed?"

### 2. Significant Decision Without Clear Answer
When the CEO faces a decision where sufficient knowledge or information is not available to decide responsibly.

**Example:** "A prospect wants to use us for healthcare leads in Germany. This has GDPR implications I cannot assess alone."

### 3. Legal or Reputational Risk
Any situation that could expose Strateon to legal liability or reputational damage.

**Example:** CLA reports a client may not have valid consent for outreach. Or government inquiry received.

### 4. C-Suite Red Flag
When any entity raises something that could materially affect the company.

**Example:** CTO reports a security vulnerability. CMO reports a competitor has copied our positioning. CFO reports revenue is below target for 2+ months.

### 5. Milestone Achieved
When something significant is reached.

**Example:** First client signed. First revenue received. Website goes live.

---

## CEO DOES NOT NEED TO NOTIFY BOARD FOR:

- Routine C-suite progress updates
- Content drafts in normal review cycle
- Day-to-day operational decisions within approved scope
- Regular weekly status reports (unless Board requests one)
- Internal critiques and direction to C-suite

---

## BOARD COMMUNICATION FORMAT

**When reaching the Board:**
- Be brief and clear
- State: what happened, what it means, what I'm proposing, what I need from the Board
- No long narratives — Board is busy
- Ask one clear question, not ten

**Board responds with:**
- Approval / Rejection / Modification
- Or: "Decide yourself, I trust your judgment"

---

## ROUTINE BOARD REPORTS

**Weekly summary — every Sunday:**
- Brief. 3–5 sentences.
- What advanced. What needs attention. Any decisions needed.
- Sent via WhatsApp.

**Monthly — during weekly review:**
- Revenue status vs. target
- Active clients
- C-suite performance summary
- Any escalations from the week

---

## ESCALATION — IMMEDIATE BOARD CONTACT

**When to call the Board urgently:**
- Data breach or suspected breach
- Government authority contact
- Client legal threat
- Any situation that could cause immediate financial or reputational damage

**How:** WhatsApp message, then follow-up with full context.
