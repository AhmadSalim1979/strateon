Begin Your Operational Evaluation

Qiyadon is operational infrastructure — not a SaaS widget. Here's how the evaluation works before any commitment.

PHASE 1 — ACTIVATION
Hours 0–72: System Setup
Duration: 72 hours

We connect your CRM, activate outbound cadence, initialize workflows, and validate integrations. This is onboarding — you are getting operational, not being evaluated.

PHASE 2 — OPERATIONAL EVALUATION
Days 1–14: Live System Evaluation
Duration: 14 days from activation completion

Your system runs live. Campaigns execute across WhatsApp, Email, and LinkedIn. You receive weekly operational reports. This is the real evaluation period — it needs the full 14 days to demonstrate value.

PHASE 3 — EXPLICIT DECISION
Day 14: Shared Assessment + Scale Option
No automatic commitment

We assess together. If Qiyadon is working for you (≥3 of 5 criteria met), you receive a Scale offer you can accept or decline. Billing begins only if you sign a new paid agreement.
