Common Questions

How is this estimate calculated?

Trucks times average weekly detention hours times 52 weeks times your detention rate gives the total detention value your fleet is likely eligible for in a year. That figure times your current collection rate gives what you are likely already collecting; the difference is the estimated uncollected amount.

Where do the default numbers come from?

The $45/hour default rate comes from the OOIDA Foundation's 2023 Detention Time Survey (the average for leased owner-operators). The 45% default collection rate comes from the American Transportation Research Institute's 2024 driver detention study, which found 94.5% of fleets charge detention fees but fewer than 50% of those invoices are actually paid.

Is this specific to my actual contracts?

No -- this calculator uses general industry averages as a starting point. Your own agreed detention rates and free-time terms vary by broker and shipper, which is exactly why applying the correct terms to each specific claim matters.
