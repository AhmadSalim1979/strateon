Product & How It Works

What is ChargeGuard and who is it for?

ChargeGuard is an automated Detention & Accessorial Charge Recovery Platform designed for small-to-midsize US trucking carriers, including owner-operators and small fleets with 1-200 trucks. It helps carriers capture and document detention and accessorial charges that they are often owed but rarely collect due to manual paperwork burdens.

How does ChargeGuard capture detention and accessorial time?

ChargeGuard captures arrival and departure timestamps at shipper and receiver facilities through carrier-provided data such as ELD/telematics feeds, geofence detection, or driver/dispatcher-submitted documentation. These timestamps are used to automatically calculate detention and accessorial charges based on the specific terms of each load, broker, or shipper.

What types of charges can ChargeGuard recover?

ChargeGuard can recover detention time and various accessorial charges, such as arrival/departure at shipper or receiver facilities, layover, TONU (Truck Ordered Not Used), and lumper fees. These charges are documented and turned into submission-ready claims or invoices.

Do I need a TMS or ELD system to use ChargeGuard?

A TMS or ELD system is not required to use ChargeGuard. The platform can work with carrier-uploaded documentation, photos, or manual entry, though it can also integrate with ELD/telematics feeds or other data sources if available.

How does ChargeGuard establish accuracy and evidence for a claim that might be disputed?

ChargeGuard establishes accuracy and evidence by capturing timestamped data at each stop, applying the specific free-time and accessorial terms that apply to that load, and generating a documented claim or invoice. This process ensures that the evidence is clear and verifiable, reducing the likelihood of disputes.

Pricing & Plans

How much does ChargeGuard cost?

ChargeGuard is priced in tiered monthly subscriptions based on fleet size. Plans start at $59/month for 5-15 trucks, $149/month for 16-50 trucks, and $349/month for 51-150 trucks. Annual billing offers a discount.

How are the pricing tiers determined?

Tiers are determined by the number of trucks in your fleet, which you declare when signing up. The Starter plan covers 5-15 trucks, Growth covers 16-50 trucks, and Scale covers 51-150 trucks.

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes, annual billing provides a discount of approximately 17% off the monthly rate. This means you'll pay $49/month ($588/year) for Starter, $124/month ($1,488/year) for Growth, and $291/month ($3,492/year) for Scale.

What happens if my fleet grows into a larger tier?

If your fleet grows into the next tier, you'll be prompted to upgrade your plan at your next renewal. Truck count is self-declared at signup, with optional verification via TMS/ELD integration.

Are there any additional costs for TMS/ELD integration?

TMS/ELD integration is included for free with the Scale plan. For Starter and Growth plans, it costs an additional $29/month per integration. This is optional and not required for core product functionality.

Data, Privacy & Security

What data does ChargeGuard need to process my detention and accessorial claims?

ChargeGuard requires load timestamps, shipper/broker terms, and supporting documentation. This data can be provided via manual entry, CSV upload, or optional ELD/telematics integration.

Does ChargeGuard contact my shippers or brokers directly?

No, ChargeGuard does not contact your shippers or brokers. You review and submit your own claims through the platform.

How does ChargeGuard maintain data confidentiality and isolation?

ChargeGuard maintains strict data isolation to ensure your information is kept confidential and separate from other carriers' data.

Who owns the data I provide to ChargeGuard?

You own all the data you provide to ChargeGuard. The data is used solely to process your detention and accessorial claims.
