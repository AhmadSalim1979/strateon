Qiyadon vs. Manual Spreadsheets for AP/AR

Founders often manage accounts payable and receivable manually in spreadsheets. Qiyadon automates this process, but there are tradeoffs to consider.

Manual Spreadsheets

Spreadsheets offer full control and customization, allowing you to track invoices, payments, and outstanding balances exactly how you want. However, this method is time-consuming and prone to human error, especially as your business grows.

Qiyadon's Autonomous AP/AR Agent

Qiyadon automates the creation and matching of vendor and customer invoices to ERPNext records without requiring founder data entry. It also proactively acts on overdue receivables and flags anomalies, freeing up your time to focus on growing your business.

Key Differences

Spreadsheets require manual data entry and tracking, while Qiyadon automates these processes. Spreadsheets offer more customization, but Qiyadon reduces the risk of human error and saves time. Currently, Qiyadon is in prototype stage and not yet available for production use.
