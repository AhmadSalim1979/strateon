Why Founders Handle AP/AR (And How to Stop)

Founders often end up handling AP/AR because it’s time-consuming and critical to cash flow. The solution? An autonomous agent that manages these tasks without founder involvement.

Published 2026-07-27

The Time Drain of AP/AR

Bookkeeping and invoicing eat up significant time for 42% of founders, according to a Bluevine study. For founder-led SMBs, this means constant back-and-forth with vendors and customers, chasing payments, and ensuring records are accurate.

Without automation, these tasks become a distraction from growing the business. Founders end up stuck in the weeds instead of focusing on strategy and scaling.

Cash Flow Risks

82% of SMB failures are linked to cash flow or late-payment issues, per a Jessie Hagen study. Manual AP/AR processes increase the risk of errors, missed payments, and delayed collections—all of which threaten financial stability.

The Autonomous Solution

An autonomous AP/AR agent handles invoicing, collections, and record-keeping without founder input. It creates and matches invoices to ERPNext records, proactively manages overdue receivables, and flags anomalies—freeing founders to focus on what matters most.

Why Founders Keep Doing It Themselves

Many founders assume AP/AR is too complex to automate or that tools require constant oversight. Others worry about accuracy or security. But an autonomous agent eliminates these concerns by operating independently and integrating seamlessly with existing systems.
