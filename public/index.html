ChargeGuard

Detention & accessorial charge recovery, built for trucking carriers

ChargeGuard is Qiyadon's first product in the field: an automated detention and accessorial charge recovery platform for small-to-midsize US trucking carriers. It applies each broker and shipper's actual agreed terms to every claim, instead of a generic industry default — turning a manual, often-skipped process into one that runs on its own.
