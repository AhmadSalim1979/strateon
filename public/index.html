Frequently Asked Questions

How does ChargeGuard help me recover detention and accessorial charges?

ChargeGuard automatically captures timestamped evidence of detention and accessorial events at each stop, applies the specific terms that apply to that load, and generates a documented claim or invoice ready for submission. This eliminates the manual paperwork burden that often prevents carriers from collecting what they're owed.

What kind of data does ChargeGuard need to work?

ChargeGuard can work with carrier-provided data such as ELD/telematics feeds, geofence detection, or driver/dispatcher-submitted timestamps and documentation. The exact data-ingestion method will be confirmed soon.

Is ChargeGuard suitable for owner-operators or only larger fleets?

ChargeGuard is designed for small-to-midsize US trucking carriers, including owner-operators and fleets with up to 150 trucks. Our pricing tiers are structured to accommodate different fleet sizes.

How does ChargeGuard handle different brokers' and shippers' terms?

ChargeGuard applies the specific free-time and accessorial terms that actually apply to each load, ensuring that you're always billing the correct amounts based on the agreements you have with each broker or shipper.

What happens if my fleet grows into a larger tier?

If your fleet grows into the next pricing tier, you'll be prompted to upgrade at your next renewal. Truck count is self-declared at signup, with optional verification via TMS/ELD integration.

How do I get started with ChargeGuard?

To learn more about ChargeGuard and how it can help your trucking business, visit our homepage or contact us directly. We're here to help you recover the detention and accessorial charges you're owed.
