Frequently Asked Questions

What is ChargeGuard and how does it work?

ChargeGuard is an automated Detention & Accessorial Charge Recovery Platform for small-to-midsize US trucking carriers. It captures timestamped evidence of detention and other accessorial events at each stop, applies the specific free-time and accessorial terms that actually apply to that load/broker/shipper, and generates a documented claim or invoice ready for submission.

How much does ChargeGuard cost?

ChargeGuard operates on a tiered flat-rate monthly subscription model banded by fleet size. Pricing starts at $59/month for 5-15 trucks, $149/month for 16-50 trucks, and $349/month for 51-150 trucks. Annual discounts are available.

Do I need to integrate my ELD or TMS to use ChargeGuard?

No, ChargeGuard can work with manual entry or CSV uploads. However, optional TMS/ELD integration is available as an add-on for Starter and Growth tiers, and is included for Scale tier customers.

What types of accessorial charges can ChargeGuard help recover?

ChargeGuard can help recover detention charges, lumper fees, and other accessorial charges that are owed but often go uncollected due to the manual process of documenting them.

How does ChargeGuard differ from other detention recovery solutions?

ChargeGuard is specifically designed for small-to-midsize trucking carriers and offers a straightforward subscription model with unlimited claim letters. Our focus is on automating the claim process to make it easier for carriers to recover what they're owed.

Is there a contract or commitment period for using ChargeGuard?

ChargeGuard operates on a month-to-month basis with no long-term contracts or commitment periods. You can cancel at any time.
