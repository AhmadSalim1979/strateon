Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this product designed for?

This product is designed for founder-led small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) who need to automate their accounts payable (AP) and accounts receivable (AR) processes.

How is this different from other bookkeeping tools?

Unlike generic bookkeeping software or tools that require founder operation, our agent fully manages AP/AR autonomously, creating and matching invoices to ERPNext records without requiring founder data entry.

What is the current state of the prototype?

The prototype is currently in development and not yet in production. It uses real capabilities like erpnext_purchase_invoice_create and erpnext_sales_invoice_create to autonomously create and match invoices.

Why is automating AP/AR important for SMBs?

Automating these processes allows founders to focus on growing their business, as bookkeeping and invoicing/collections are significant pain points that consume a lot of time.

What evidence supports the need for this product?

Studies show that bookkeeping is a significant time drain for 42% of founders, and 82% of SMB failures are traced to cash-flow/late-payment issues.

How does the agent handle overdue receivables?

The agent proactively acts on overdue receivables and flags anomalies, ensuring that cash flow issues are minimized.
