Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a discount for paying annually?

Yes, you'll save roughly 17% by choosing annual billing instead of monthly.

Do I need a TMS or ELD system to use ChargeGuard?

No, the core product works with manual entry or CSV upload alone. TMS/ELD integration is an optional convenience, priced at +$29/month on Starter and Growth tiers, and included free at Scale.

What happens if my fleet grows into a larger tier's truck range?

You'll be prompted to upgrade at your next renewal. There are no surprise mid-cycle charges.

How are the tiers decided?

Tiers are based on the truck count you declare at signup.

Why do free-time and rates differ from one broker or shipper to another, and how does ChargeGuard handle that?

Each customer's agreed terms are stored once and applied automatically to every future claim for that customer, instead of using one generic rule for everyone.
