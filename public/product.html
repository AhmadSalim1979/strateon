Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the target segment for this autonomous AP/AR agent?

The target segment is founder-led SMBs broadly, not sector-specific. These businesses often struggle with time-consuming bookkeeping and invoicing tasks.

How is this agent different from other bookkeeping tools?

Unlike generic bookkeeping software or tools that require founder operation, Qiyadon's agent fully manages AP/AR autonomously. It uses real erpnext_purchase_invoice_create and erpnext_sales_invoice_create capabilities to create and match invoices to ERPNext records without founder data entry.

What problem does this agent solve?

Founders of SMBs spend significant time on bookkeeping and invoicing/collections, which are major pain points. Automating these processes allows founders to focus on growing their business.

What evidence supports the need for this agent?

A Bluevine study found that bookkeeping is a significant time drain for 42% of founders, and a Jessie Hagen study traced 82% of SMB failures to cash-flow/late-payment issues.

What is the current state of the prototype?

The autonomous AP/AR agent is in the prototype stage. It has been implemented and tested for creating and matching invoices to ERPNext records autonomously, as well as proactively acting on overdue receivables and flagging anomalies.
