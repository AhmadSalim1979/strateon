Care

Care, explained clearly.

Proposed treatment categories for the practice to review. Each is Subject to clinic confirmation and only the approved list will appear on the finished site.

How treatment pages would work. Each confirmed treatment would have its own page explaining who it's for, what happens, how long it takes, recovery, alternatives and how fees are discussed, reviewed and signed off by a named clinician. See the template, using check-ups as the example.

Everyday and preventive care

Check-ups and cleaning

An examination of teeth, gums and soft tissues, with professional cleaning where needed, and advice on how often to return.

Fillings

Repairing a tooth affected by decay or minor damage, and explaining the filling materials that suit that tooth.

Gum care

Checking for gum inflammation and disease, cleaning below the gum line where appropriate, and a plan for keeping gums healthy at home.

Children's dentistry

Dental care for children, with guidance for parents on brushing, diet and developing teeth.

Restoring teeth

Subject to clinic confirmation

Crowns

A cap that covers and protects a weakened or heavily restored tooth.

Subject to clinic confirmation

Root canal care

Treating infection inside a tooth so that the tooth can be kept, often followed by a crown.

Subject to clinic confirmation

Extractions

Removing a tooth that cannot be saved, and discussing options for the space afterwards.

Subject to clinic confirmation

Implants

Replacing a missing tooth with an implant-supported crown where assessment shows it is suitable.

Alignment and appearance

Subject to clinic confirmation

Orthodontics or aligners

Straightening teeth with braces or clear aligners after an assessment of bite, gums and expectations.

Subject to clinic confirmation

Whitening

Clinician-supervised whitening after a check that teeth and gums are healthy enough for it. Whitening does not change the colour of crowns or fillings.
