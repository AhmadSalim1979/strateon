Your visit

Your first visit, step by step.

A sample first-visit journey, shared for the clinic to review and adapt to how it actually works.

The journey

From request to consultation.

A request is not a booking. If the clinic adds appointment requests, a request would only tell the clinic when you'd prefer to come. An appointment is confirmed only when the clinic confirms a date and time with you.

Proposed experience, for clinic review

  1. Before you arrive

    The clinic arranges a time with you. The proposal is that patients are told who they'll see and roughly how long to allow.

  2. Arriving and settling in

    A short welcome at reception. How medical history is collected and kept would follow the clinic's own confirmed process, never this website.

  3. A conversation first

    The visit starts with what brought you in and what matters to you, whether that's pain, a worry or a check-up you've been putting off.

  4. A careful examination

    A look at your teeth, gums and mouth, with X-rays where the dentist judges them clinically useful.

  5. Options, in plain language

    What the dentist found and the realistic options, including doing nothing. You can ask what each involves and costs, and take time to think before agreeing to treatment.

How to prepare

  • A list of any medicines you take, including doses.
  • Details of any allergies or medical conditions.
  • Previous dental X-rays or treatment notes, if you have them.
  • Your questions, written down so nothing is forgotten.
  • For children: check whether a parent or guardian needs to attend.

General suggestions. Please don't send any of this through this website.

Questions you can ask

  • What did you find, and how urgent is it?
  • What are my options, including waiting?
  • What does each option involve, and how many visits?
  • What will it cost in total? Can I have that in writing?
  • What happens if I decide not to go ahead?
