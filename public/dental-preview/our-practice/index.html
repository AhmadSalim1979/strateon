About the practice

Dr. Asif Niaz Arain & Associates

Dr. Asif Niaz Arain & Associates Dental Professionals bears the name of Dr. Asif Niaz Arain. This page introduces the approach to care proposed for the practice, and remembers Dr. Arain.

Our approach

How the practice could describe its care.

Proposed wording, for clinic review

Proposed wording for the family and clinical team to approve, adapt or replace before it describes how the practice works.

Listening first

Every visit begins with the patient's own account: what they have noticed, what worries them, and what they hope for. Time spent listening shapes everything that follows.

Clear explanations

Findings are explained in everyday language, with the chance to see what the dentist sees. Clinical terms are used only when they help, and always explained.

Informed decisions

Patients hear the realistic options, including doing nothing, with what each involves and what it costs. Decisions are theirs to make, in their own time where the situation allows.

Continuity

Good dental care is a long conversation, not a single appointment. Follow-up is planned sensibly, and prevention matters as much as treatment.

In memory

Remembering Dr. Asif Niaz Arain

The practice bears the name of Dr. Asif Niaz Arain, who died on 9 March 2024. He is not available for appointments.

A tribute to Dr. Arain will appear here once his family has reviewed and approved it. His portrait will be shown only with the family's approval.

Draft for family review

Public reporting at the time of his death (Dental News, article dated 10 March 2024) described Dr. Arain as:

  • Former Vice President of the Asia Pacific Dental Federation
  • Senior member of the Pakistan Dental Association
  • Involved in national and international dental conferences

That reporting concerns Dr. Arain's professional life. It does not describe how this practice began or who has led it since, and this page makes no such claims. These points are shown so the family can confirm, correct or replace them.
