The approach

Listening, explaining, deciding together.

The approach proposed for the practice, for the family and clinical team to shape before it describes how the practice works.

i.

Listen first

A visit that begins with what you want to talk about, whether that's a specific worry or simply a check-up.

ii.

Explain the options

What the dentist found, the realistic options and what each involves and costs, in everyday language.

iii.

Decide together

Time for questions, and no treatment until you have agreed to it.
