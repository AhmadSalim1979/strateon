Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I contact ChargeGuard?

Our team will respond to your inquiry and provide information tailored to your fleet's needs. We'll guide you through the next steps to get started with ChargeGuard.

What information should I have ready when I reach out?

Having details about your fleet size, current detention and accessorial charge recovery process, and any specific challenges you face will help us provide the most relevant information.

How does ChargeGuard handle my data?

ChargeGuard is designed to work with data you already have, such as ELD/telematics feeds or manual timestamps. We prioritize data security and privacy in all our processes.

Can ChargeGuard integrate with my existing systems?

ChargeGuard can work with various data sources, including manual entry, CSV uploads, and optional TMS/ELD integrations. We'll discuss the best setup for your fleet during our initial conversation.
