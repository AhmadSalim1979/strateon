ERPNext Integration: How Qiyadon Automates AP/AR Inside ERPNext

Yes, Qiyadon integrates directly with ERPNext. It uses the erpnext_purchase_invoice_create and erpnext_sales_invoice_create capabilities to autonomously create and match invoices to ERPNext records.

Published 2026-07-27

How Qiyadon Creates and Matches Invoices

Qiyadon uses ERPNext’s native capabilities to create purchase and sales invoices autonomously. It matches these invoices to existing ERPNext records without requiring founder data entry.

Autonomous AP/AR Processing

The agent handles accounts payable (AP) and accounts receivable (AR) entirely on its own. It proactively acts on overdue receivables and flags anomalies in the process.

No Founder Intervention Required

Unlike generic bookkeeping tools, Qiyadon doesn’t require founders to operate it. Once set up, it fully manages AP/AR autonomously within ERPNext.

Proactive Anomaly Detection

The agent flags anomalies in invoicing and receivables, helping founders catch issues early without manual review.

Focus on Your Business, Not Bookkeeping

By automating AP/AR inside ERPNext, Qiyadon frees founders from time-consuming bookkeeping tasks, allowing them to focus on growth.
