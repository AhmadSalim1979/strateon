# SEO, local search and AI-discovery handover

Nothing here guarantees a ranking, a map-pack position or inclusion in
Google AI Overviews / AI Mode. The aim is accurate, consistent and useful
information that search systems (and patients) can understand.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)
- [ ] Claim or verify the existing profile for "Dr. Asif Niaz Arain & Associates Dental Professionals". Search for duplicates and request merges instead of creating a new one.
- [ ] The practice owner is Primary Owner. Agencies get Manager access only.
- [ ] Primary category **Dentist**. Add secondary categories only for approved services.
- [ ] Name exactly as on signage. No keywords added to the name.
- [ ] Address, pin, phone, hours (including Friday prayer break, Ramadan and holidays) identical to the website.
- [ ] Website link points at the clinic domain home page (or `/contact/`), **never** at qiyadon.com.
- [ ] Authentic photos: exterior with signage, reception, treatment room, team (with consent).
- [ ] Description written from approved facts. No superlatives or outcome claims.

## Reviews (legitimate management only)
- Ask every patient in the same neutral way (for example a QR card at reception or a message after the visit). Don't pick and choose, and don't offer incentives.
- Reply to reviews without confirming that the reviewer is a patient or discussing their treatment.
- Never write, buy or place reviews, and never mark up reviews on the site as structured data.

## Search Console
- [ ] Verify the clinic domain as a **Domain property** (DNS TXT record).
- [ ] Submit `https://<clinic-domain>/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Check Coverage/Pages for unexpected `noindex` after launch. The production build should have none on approved pages.
- [ ] Do **not** add the qiyadon.com preview to any Search Console property for the clinic.

## Consistent identity
- Name, address and phone should be identical on the website, GBP, Facebook page, and directory listings such as citysearch.pk. Ask directories to correct outdated phone numbers and hours once verified.
- Link the verified Facebook page from the site footer once it is confirmed to be clinic-controlled.

## Content that serves patients and AI answers
- One page per approved treatment and per current clinician. Answer the questions patients actually ask: who it's for, what happens, how long it takes, recovery, alternatives, and how fees are discussed.
- Mention Clifton and Karachi naturally in place descriptions. Don't create near-duplicate pages for each neighbourhood.
- Every health page shows the reviewing clinician's name and review date, plus its sources.
- FAQs on the page are fine for patients, but don't expect FAQ rich results.

## Measurement (baseline before launch)
- Primary metric: **qualified appointment requests**, meaning calls, WhatsApp chats and form requests that lead to a real booking. Track them in a simple log kept by the practice.
- If analytics is added, use a privacy-respecting tool with no session recording on the site. Never send form contents or health information to analytics.
- GBP Insights: calls, direction requests and website clicks per month.
